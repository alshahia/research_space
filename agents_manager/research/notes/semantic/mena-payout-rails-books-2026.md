# Semantic note: MENA (IQ/SY) book-platform payout reality - 2026-08-12

When advising authors from Iraq/Syria about book-selling platforms, the binding constraint is PAYOUT RAILS, not publishing:

1. Verified 2026-08-12: KDP payment-options country table has NO Iraq/Syria (MENA entry: Jordan only); Google Play Books seller/payment table has NO Iraq/Syria (EG/SA/AE/KW closed ★ to new sellers); Apple Books is absent for Iraq and unlisted for Syria, GCC is public-domain-only; PayPal country list has NO Iraq/Syria.
2. Consequence: any platform paying through PayPal or Stripe (Leanpub, Payhip, Substack, Ream, Gumroad partial, Ko-fi, Kotebee Books own-gateway) is effectively closed unless the author has a foreign PayPal/Stripe - the "platform accepts me" test is weaker than "rail accepts my bank".
3. Aggregators (D2D, StreetLib, Lulu, IngramSpark, PublishDrive) publish no author-country lists; their Payoneer/EFT rails are the only plausible path - always FLAG "verify at signup" instead of assuming.
4. Fresh 2026 facts that old sources miss: KDP 70% band now $2.99–$12.99 (2026-07-07); Smashwords became store-only Jan 2026 (publishing via D2D); Wattpad Paid Stories dead → Originals (invite-only, no Arabic); Substack = 10% + Stripe 2.9%+$0.30+0.7% billing.
5. Research pattern that worked: platform help centers (zendesk/hc) are fetch-friendly; marketing pages (ko-fi.com, payhip.com) 400-block; official country tables (support.google.com 2987594, support.apple.com HT204411, paypal country-worldwide) are the gold standard for eligibility claims.

Sanctions reframe (verified 2026-08-12, Angle C T-2026-08-12-002): OFAC removed comprehensive Syria sanctions 2025-07-01 (EO 14312; only targeted PAARSS 31 CFR 569 remains) and removed Iraqi sanctions regs in 2010 (only targeted 31 CFR 576).
