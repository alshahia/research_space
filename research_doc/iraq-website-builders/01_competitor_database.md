## Key Takeaways

1. **No Iraqi self-serve drag-and-drop website builder exists.** Every Iraqi "competitor" is either a custom-development agency or an international platform.
2. **IQ Hosting is the closest thing** to our offering — they include a website builder with hosting — but it's an add-on, not a dedicated platform.
3. **Sitee IQ** is the closest in **pricing model** — they offer one-time payment for pre-built sites starting at $250.
4. **Wuilt is the sleeping giant** — an Arabic-first, free, drag-and-drop builder from Egypt with $2M in funding. If they enter Iraq, they become the #1 competitor overnight.
5. **The freelance market is huge but fragmented** — thousands of individual developers competing on price across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Telegram.
