## 1. W-8BEN mechanics

- **Who files:** non-US individuals earning **US-sourced royalties**. US platforms require the W-8BEN from non-US creators: KDP (the tax profile generates the W-8 at signup), D2D (online W-8 interview required before publishing), Lulu (W-8BEN for non-US), Apple (US tax form mandatory for **ALL** partners), Google (US tax info required when selling in US/USD). [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200620010, https://www.draft2digital.com/faq/, https://help.lulu.com/en/support/solutions/articles/64000255468-tax-and-withholding-the-basics, https://itunespartner.apple.com/books/support/7-required-tax-forms, https://support.google.com/books/partner/answer/6009580, 2026-08-12]
- **Line 2 = country of citizenship; Line 3 = permanent residence (tax residence).** The form asks both, and the withholding rate is what matters — for this user it is 30% either way (next bullet). [https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben, 2026-08-12]
- **The rate is 30% either way — no treaty exists.** US-source royalties to foreign persons are taxed at **30% by default**, withheld at source under IRC §1441. There is **no US–Iraq and no US–Syria income tax treaty** (both absent from the IRS treaty A–Z list; Egypt has one) — so 30% applies whether Line 2/3 say Syria or Iraq. [https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben, https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z, 2026-08-12]
- KDP's own wording: "The default withholding rate is 30%, but you may be eligible for a reduced rate if your country of tax residence has a tax treaty with the U.S." — the treaty path does not exist for Iraq or Syria. [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274750, 2026-08-12]
- **TIN:** KDP accepts your country's TIN (an Iraq TIN), an **ITIN**, or an **EIN**. [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274750, 2026-08-12]
- **Expiry:** the W-8BEN expires; after expiry the rate **resets to 30%** (the default foreign-person rate without a valid W-8). Keep it current. [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274750, 2026-08-12]

## 3. Net-royalty math (the 30% applied — worked examples)

Every example shows its arithmetic step by step. **Delivery figures are KDP's published per-file rates, but the file size is an assumption** — every delivery line is labeled **estimate**. Withholding is applied to the US-sourced royalty amount (the 30% default).

### 3.1 KDP — $9.99 Arabic eBook, US-sourced sale

| Step | Math | Result |
|------|------|--------|
| List price | — | $9.99 |
| 70% royalty (70% band $2.99–$12.99, eff. 2026-07-07) | 9.99 × 0.70 | $6.99 |
| Delivery fee (**estimate** — KDP per-file: $0.06/MB US, $0.15/MB elsewhere; assumed ~8 MB Arabic EPUB at the US rate) | 8 MB × $0.06/MB | ≈ $0.50 |
| Royalty after delivery | 6.99 − 0.50 | $6.49 |
| 30% US withholding on the US-sourced royalty | 6.49 × 0.30 | ≈ $1.95 |
| **Net royalty** | 6.49 − 1.95 | **≈ $4.54** |

Sensitivity (labeled estimate): the **≈ $4.40** headline in the master synthesis (§1) is the same formula with a heavier delivery assumption — e.g. ≈ $0.70 delivery (larger file): 6.99 − 0.70 = 6.29 → 6.29 × 0.30 = 1.89 → 6.29 − 1.89 = **≈ $4.40**. Both results are estimates; the delivery line is the only assumption that moves the last dollar. Before currency conversion. [master synthesis §1; https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560, 2026-08-12]

### 3.2 D2D — $9.99 ebook, US-sourced sale (≈70% net typical)

### 3.3 Lulu — the one documented exception (US-delivered sales only)

Lulu withholds 30% (foreign, no W-8BEN) **only on sales delivered within the US** for Lulu-ISBN royalties; own-ISBN revenue is "Other Revenue" with **no US withholding**; 1042-S issued for non-US. Lulu is the only platform in the research documenting this narrower scope — D2D/KDP/Apple document the 30% default + treaty path that does not exist for IQ/SY. ### Margin facts that apply to all examples

- The 30% applies to **US-sourced royalties** from KDP/D2D/Apple/Google; Lulu is the documented delivery-based exception (angle-eligibility risks; canonical "Tax (verified)").
- Price accordingly: with 30% withheld, a $9.99 book nets roughly **$4–5 per US-sourced sale** before currency conversion (3.1–3.3) — the canonical margin fact (master synthesis §1).
