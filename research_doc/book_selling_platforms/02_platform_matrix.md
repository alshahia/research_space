### 1. Amazon KDP

**Setup:** **$0 free.** Amazon account → KDP signup → legal name (individual or corporation) + mailing address (tax/payment) → identity verification (ID photos) → tax profile (W-8BEN for non-US; TIN needed for treaty benefits; 30% default without treaty) → two-step verification for bank entry. No published review/approval time. [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200620010, GHASDBMPJDXLKXBC, 2026-08-12]
**Publish:** eBook Word/DOCX/EPUB/KPF; quality gates (title page match, TOC, RTL direction for Arabic); no ISBN needed for Kindle eBook; **Arabic paperback not currently available** (eBook only). Print: PDF interior, trim sizes (English/other). KDP Select = 90-day Kindle-store exclusivity, auto-renews. [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GUQT4C8J6RR6V8TY, G200798990, GD9PMU58BV24QFZ7, 2026-08-12]
**Audience:** No official MAU/store-visit figure published (FLAG). Citable scale proxy: KDP Select/KU global fund **$67.0M for June 2026**; "all authors regardless of where they live are eligible" (eligibility ≠ payable). [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200798990, GD9PMU58BV24QFZ7, 2026-08-12]
**Royalty:** 70% option **$2.99–$12.99** list (Amazon.com; equivalent local bands) **minus delivery fees** ($0.06/MB US, $0.15/MB elsewhere); 35% option $0.99–$200, no delivery fee. **Margin: 30% US withholding on US-sourced royalties** — KDP documents "the default withholding rate is 30%"; no US–Iraq / US–Syria treaty, so 30% either way; W-8BEN expiry resets to 30%; TIN = Iraq TIN/ITIN/EIN accepted. [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560, G201274750; https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben, https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z, 2026-08-12]
**Payout:** EFT direct deposit / wire / check only — no PayPal, no Amazon Payments. **Iraq: NO. Syria: NO** — not in the Payment Options country table (only Jordan from MENA); for unlisted countries "Other" = **paper check by mail (USD)**. PSP route: **Payoneer and Wise both participating KDP PSPs** (as of 2026-08-01 payments to non-participating PSPs stopped; PSP eligibility per-account). [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GJD7DFFX6X88AK46, G6GLVRHVQZY4V4FX, GD9NN4LS8ZDU8XZ7, 2026-08-12]
**Verdict (IQ/SY):** ⚠️ conditional — needs Payoneer/Wise eligibility (Test A, D1) or check-by-mail (technically available; mail reliability user-side). KDP never asks nationality in help docs; the W-8 asks citizenship (30% either way). ⚠️ **Callout:** 70% band $2.99–$12.99 eff. **2026-07-07**. [canonical row 1; angle-eligibility verdict matrix, 2026-08-12]
**Arabic:** eBook yes — RTL mandatory (LTR files rejected), tashkeel/spacing quality gates, Arabic metadata must match language selection; paperback NO. amazon.ae/.sa are **NOT KDP payment marketplaces**; Arabic help page documents local-currency wire for KSA/OM/BH/KW/MA bank accounts. [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GUQT4C8J6RR6V8TY, GJD7DFFX6X88AK46, 2026-08-12]
**Sources:** kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560 · GJD7DFFX6X88AK46 · GUQT4C8J6RR6V8TY · G200620010 · G200798990 · GD9PMU58BV24QFZ7 · G201274750 · G6GLVRHVQZY4V4FX · GD9NN4LS8ZDU8XZ7 — all accessed 2026-08-12.

**Publish:** EPUB/PDF upload; no ISBN required; no exclusivity; content policies; pre-orders; territories configurable; audiobooks can be uploaded. [https://support.google.com/books/partner/answer/6009580, 14164701, 2026-08-12]

Seller sign-ups: EG/SA/AE/KW marked ★ = "select partners only; Google doesn't accept new partners". No MAU published (FLAG). [https://support.google.com/books/partner/answer/2987594, 2026-08-12]

No fees. **Margin: 30% US withholding on US-sourced royalties** (no treaty either way; Google requires US tax info when selling in US/USD). **Arabic:** Play Books sells Arabic-language titles; Arabic RTL EPUB supported; Arabic UI for MENA buyers; new Arabic-market sellers closed to new partners (★). [https://support.google.com/books/partner/answer/2987594, 2026-08-12]

### 3. Apple Books for Authors

**Setup:** **$0 free.** Apple Account → iTunes Connect signup → publisher type (Individual/Organization) → email verification → agreement (free or paid) → banking + tax info (IRS verification "takes a few days"; W-8BEN/W-8BEN-E/W-8ECI for non-US). Application-based; no open self-serve publishing until approved. [https://authors.apple.com/support/3967-create-itunes-connect-account, https://itunespartner.apple.com/books/support/7-required-tax-forms, 2026-08-12]
**Publish:** EPUB (or Word via conversion tools); no ISBN required (Apple assigns); no exclusivity; no price matching; free books allowed; no file delivery fees. [https://authors.apple.com/, 2026-08-12]
**Audience:** "millions of customers" (marketing claim, unaudited); no MAU published (FLAG). **Iraq — Apple Books NOT available at all; Syria — not listed; KSA/UAE/Jordan/Kuwait/Türkiye/Qatar — "Public domain books only"** (commercial ebooks cannot be purchased there). [https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204411, 2026-08-12]
**Royalty:** **70% flat on every ebook regardless of price**, no delivery fees, no third-party ads, no paid placement. **Margin: 30% US withholding on US-sourced royalties** — Apple documents the 30% default + treaty path; no US–IQ/US–SY treaty exists, so 30% either way. [https://authors.apple.com/; canonical tax section; https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben, https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z, 2026-08-12]
**Payout:** monthly, ~45 days after month end, bank transfer; banking added via a **Bank Country dropdown** in iTunes Connect with supported currencies; per-country payout list **not published** → verify at signup. **Iraq: effectively NO** (no storefront, no banking). **Syria: NO** (not listed in Apple's availability tables). [https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204411, https://itunespartner.apple.com/books/articles/set-up-banking-information-in-itunes-connect-2705, 2026-08-12]
**Verdict (IQ/SY):** ⚠️/🔶 — residence-based; US tax form mandatory for ALL partners; Bank Country dropdown content for Iraq/Syria unverified → **verify at signup**. ⚠️ **Callout:** Apple Ireland exporter-status (no Irish VAT on Apple purchases) **ended April 2026**. [canonical row 3; angle-eligibility verdict matrix + tax, 2026-08-12]
**Arabic:** EPUB RTL renders (Apple Books renders RTL EPUBs); Arabic metadata supported in iTunes Connect. But commercial Arabic sales in KSA/UAE are blocked at purchase level (public-domain-only). **Format yes, market no.** [https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204411, 2026-08-12]
**🔶 verify at signup** — Bank Country dropdown content for Iraq/Syria (checklist row in 99_appendix).
