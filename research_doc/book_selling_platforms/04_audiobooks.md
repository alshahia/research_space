## 3. Audiobook production note ($0 budget — what the "free" actually covers)

- **Narration cost is user-side and uncitable.** Production/studio/narrator pricing was not researched, varies by market, and no figure exists in the research files — say so rather than guess. **The "$0" label in this dossier applies to platform setup only**, not to producing the audio itself.
- **AI narration: not recommended without flagging platform policies.** Do not assume any platform accepts AI-generated narration:
  - Kotobee (cross-ref from Task 001) has **an AI clause in its own ToS** — carried over as canonical flag 11, verify at signup.
  - **ACX and Spotify/Voices by INaudio policies on AI narration must be verified at signup** — the research files contain no specific AI-narration policy statements for ACX or Voices by INaudio, so no specific claim is made here.
- Budget reality: a $0 budget covers platform setup (ACX, Voices by INaudio registrations); the audio itself costs either paid production, a revenue-share narration deal (ACX's model supports royalty-share narrators — subject to the same payout rails), or AI narration **only where a platform explicitly permits it and its terms are verified**.
