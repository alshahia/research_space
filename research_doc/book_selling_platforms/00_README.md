## Verdict in one page (the answer to the user's question)

Legend: ✅ documented-eligible · ⚠️ conditional (depends on a rail/bank test) · ❌ blocked per official docs · 🔶 unverified — verify at signup.

1. **Nationality is not the blocker.** The US revoked comprehensive Syria sanctions effective **2025-07-01** (EO 14312; only targeted PAARSS 31 CFR 569 remains) and Iraq has not been comprehensively sanctioned since 2010. A Syrian national residing in KRG is **NOT OFAC-blocked by nationality or residence** — full evidence in `01_eligibility_and_rails.md` §1.
2. **The rails are the gate.** Payout availability decides the open set of platforms: Stripe ❌ and PayPal ❌ per official evidence; Payoneer 🔶 = the single decisive test; Wise 🔶 = participating KDP PSP; bank EFT depends on the KRG bank accepting SWIFT (step 0, user-side); check-by-mail ⚠️ exists as a technical fallback (KDP "Other" USD check, Lulu quarterly $20 min, D2D $100 min).
3. **Test-first tier:** KDP (Arabic eBook, 70% band $2.99–$12.99 eff. 2026-07-07), Draft2Digital (bilingual wide EN/AR, $20 activation), Kotobee (Arabic-native storefront, 100% royalties "limited time"), Lulu (check/PayPal), Kobo (EFT) — all ⚠️ conditional on the two rail tests, each with a full sheet in `02_platform_matrix.md`.
4. **Blocked today:** Google Play Books (seller + payments lists), IngramSpark (author-country residency gate), and the Stripe-rail platforms (Substack, Ko-fi, Payhip, Ream) plus Leanpub (PayPal rail) and Patreon (unless Payoneer covers the KRG residence) — ❌ per official lists, not by nationality.
5. **First action: the two 10-minute tests** (decision point D1 — `01_eligibility_and_rails.md` §3 and `05_action_plan.md` Step 1): Test A = Payoneer registration with the KRG address (decisive), Test B = PayPal signup probe. They re-classify ~60% of the matrix, and the action plan works on either branch.

Bottom line (from 01): **the gate is money movement, not nationality.**

**Recommended stack (per 02 sheets + 05):** KDP Arabic eBook + D2D bilingual EN/AR wide + Kotobee storefront + negotiated Arabic channels (Neelwafurat = best fit; Rufoof/Abjjad via the outreach templates in 03) + ACX for English audiobooks (Arabic audio deferred until a channel clears verification — 04).

**Margin fact (per 06):** 30% US withholding on US-sourced royalties — no US–Iraq and no US–Syria income tax treaty exists, so the rate is 30% either way; the recommendation is to claim **Iraq (KRG) as tax residence** on the W-8BEN for internal consistency.

**Free-first:** the stack is $0 to set up except D2D's $20 activation; paid tiers (StreetLib $99/yr, PublishDrive plans, D2D $12/yr maintenance) are later-only options, not part of the path (05).
