## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS FOR IRAQ

### 🇮🇶 What Makes a Product Sell in Iraq

1. **Solves a real problem** — AC remote holder, cooler knob, battery cover
2. **Low price point** — $3-8 items sell fastest (impulse buy range)
3. **Culturally relevant** — Arabic calligraphy, Islamic patterns, hookah accessories
4. **Durable** — PETG/ABS not PLA for anything exposed to heat
5. **Instagram-worthy** — Visual products with good photos sell themselves

### ✅ Beginner Tips
- Start with **PLA** — cheapest, easiest, good for 80% of home products
- Switch to **PETG** for anything that goes in a car or near a kitchen stove
- Keep your **first 10 prints small** (< 2h each) to learn your printer
- **Batch print** identical items to save time
- Account for **15% waste** (failed prints, test prints) in your pricing

### 🚫 Products to Avoid in Iraq
- Large prints (8+ hours) — too risky for beginners, too slow for profit
- Anything requiring resin printer (toxic, messy, less accessible)
- Licensed characters (Mickey, Marvel) — legal trouble
- Extremely niche hobby items — market too small
- Anything that needs assembly instructions longer than 30 seconds
