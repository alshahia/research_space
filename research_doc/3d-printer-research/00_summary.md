### 2. How to Use — Quick Start

The workflow is: **Idea → 3D Model (TinkerCAD/Blender) → Slicer (Cura/OrcaSlicer/Bambu Studio) → G-code → Print → Post-process**. Key beginner tips: start with PLA, master bed leveling (modern printers auto-level), and always watch the first layer. ### 4. Recycled Materials — Feasibility

**Yes, recycled plastic works in 3D printing.** PET bottles are the most practical feedstock in Iraq (widely available). A desktop extruder system costs $1,400-3,000 and becomes cost-effective if you use >10 kg filament/month. Nylon recycling is also possible but requires higher temperatures and meticulous drying. For occasional users, buying recycled filament (Reflow, Filamentive) is more practical than DIY.

## 🧠 Key Conclusions

1. **Iraq has a functioning 3D printer market** with 5+ local retailers, 12+ available printer models, and reasonable pricing (30-40% premium vs global MSRP).

2. **Best starter printer for an Iraqi beginner:** **Bambu Lab A1 at 730K IQD** — or if budget is tight, **Creality Ender-3 V3 SE at 305K IQD**.

3. **A business is viable** as a side hustle starting with ~$500 investment. Focus on custom/repair parts, not commodity prints.

4. **Recycling is feasible** for high-volume users or environmental projects. PET bottles are the best feedstock in Iraq. DIY extrusion requires $1,400-3,000 equipment investment.

5. **PLA is the best starting material** ($15-25/kg, easiest to print). PETG is the next step up. ABS needs an enclosed printer.
