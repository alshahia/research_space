# 3D Printed Repair Parts, Automotive & Tech Accessories for Iraq

## Executive Summary

This report covers **40+ products** across three high-demand categories for the Iraq market. The key insight: **functional/repair items command 10-50x material cost** because they solve specific problems — broken parts, missing pieces, organization. Iraq's car market is dominated by Toyota (#1), Kia (#2), and Hyundai (#3), with significant Nissan and MG presence. The hot climate (50°C+ summer) means PETG/ABS/ASA are required for automotive interior parts; PLA deforms. Tech accessories have year-round demand. Repair parts are perpetual — broken stuff doesn't care about seasons.

All prices in USD and IQD (1 USD ≈ 1,310 IQD as of mid-2026). Material cost estimate: ~$20/kg for PLA/PETG, ~$25/kg for ABS/ASA, ~$30/kg for TPU.

## Key Selling Strategies for Iraq

1. **Partner with local repair shops** — Electronics repair shops, car mechanics, opticians all deal with customers who need small replacement parts. Offer them wholesale pricing (50% of retail) and they'll sell your prints for you. This is the fastest path to volume.

2. **Facebook Marketplace + OpenSooq** — The Amazon of Iraq. Post clear photos with prices in IQD. Join car brand-specific Facebook groups (Toyota Hilux Iraq, Kia Iraq Owners, etc.) and post relevant products there.

3. **Instagram for visual products** — Phone stands, Apple Watch stands, custom phone cases, headphone stands. Use before/after shots and lifestyle photos. Iraqi Instagram has a growing 3D printing community.

4. **Bundle for higher average order** — "Car Starter Pack" (phone mount + seat gap filler + tire caps) for $18. "Repair Pack" (knobs + battery covers + cabinet clips assorted) for $10.

5. **PETG/ABS is mandatory for automotive** — Warn customers that PLA deforms in cars. Use PETG as default for anything that goes in a vehicle or outdoors. Charge more for ASA (best UV resistance).

6. **Iraq pays cash-on-delivery** — Be prepared for COD model on Facebook/OpenSooq. Build trust with a page/shop that has reviews.

7. **Print time vs profit** — Don't tie up your printer for 8-14 hours on a monitor stand when you could print 10 battery covers in the same time and make more money. Prioritize small, fast, high-margin items.

8. **License plate design strategy** — Offer pro-Iraq designs (flag colors, map of Iraq). These sell well for patriotic customization.

9. **Seasonal timing** — Summer (May-Sept) is peak for car accessories, sunglass holders, water bottle cup adapters. Pre-Ramadan (Feb-March) is peak for home repairs as families prepare homes.

10. **The "I can't find this anywhere" premium** — The #1 reason people overpay for 3D printed repair parts: they've searched for weeks, can't find the part, and your $5 knob saves them from buying a $200 stove.
