# 3D Printed Home Decor & Kitchen Products for Iraq

## Executive Summary

Home decor and kitchen products are a strong market for 3D printing in Iraq. The Iraq home decor market was estimated at USD 332M in 2025 with 5.2% CAGR growth. Iraqi consumers increasingly blend traditional Islamic design elements with contemporary styles. Products with Islamic geometric patterns, Arabic calligraphy, and mashrabiya-inspired designs have the highest cultural resonance. Kitchen functional items (measuring spoons, bag clips, spice racks) offer the fastest print times and highest margins. For the Iraq market, focus on: gold, white, black, and matte finishes; heat-resistant materials (PETG) for kitchen items near stoves; and compact sizes suitable for smaller city apartments. The biggest selling channels in Iraq are Facebook (Facebook Marketplace & groups), OpenSooq, Instagram, and local bazaars — not Etsy.

**Key insights:**
- Iraq home decor market: $332M (2025), growing at 5.2% CAGR
- Average IQD-to-USD rate used: ~1,450 IQD per 1 USD
- Filament cost estimate: $15-20/kg for PLA (retail Iraq), ~$0.015-0.02/g
- Best margins: small kitchen items (500%+ markup), Islamic wall art (300%+), lanterns (400%+)
- PLA average $20.73/kg, PETG $18.15/kg (US retail); Iraq import prices may be 20-30% higher
- Cash-on-delivery is standard in Iraq — factor this into payment setup
