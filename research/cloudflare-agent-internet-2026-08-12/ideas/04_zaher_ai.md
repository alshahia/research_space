## What Zaher.AI is (one-line)
"AI Visibility Infrastructure for Arabic-First Brands" — the first end-to-end platform that **audits, fixes, and proves** Arabic brand visibility inside ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Meta AI, Microsoft Copilot, and Google AI Overviews.

## Why this is directly relevant to the Cloudflare project
Zaher.AI is essentially **Greg's "Agent Readiness for Businesses" idea, already in production**, with two important axes the Greg idea didn't emphasize:
1. **Arabic-first** — native Arabic NLP, dialect handling, MENA pricing (EGP primary, SAR, USD reference), MENA-targeted content
2. **E-commerce vertical** — "Agentic Shopping" module is a marquee feature (winning the AI shopping shelf inside ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google AI)

## Strategic positioning
- **Region:** MENA (Egypt HQ, phone +2010 7080 3070)
- **Language:** Arabic-first, English-ready
- **ICP:** Brands, agencies, publishers across MENA
- **Channel:** Direct + Agency partnerships (/agencies)
- **Differentiation:** Native Arabic NLP + e-commerce "Agentic Shopping" + MENA pricing in EGP
- **Product strategy:** 5 modules LIVE (audit + analyze + optimize + write + SEO) → 3 modules SOON (shopping + analytics + autonomous agent) — clearly building toward the full Greg Isenberg vision

## What this brief should NOT be confused with
- The "Expert Archives as Agent Tools" idea (Idea 3) — Zaher is **brand visibility for AI search**, not packaging expert content for agents.
- The "Niche Data Refinery" idea (Idea 1) — Zaher is **outbound monitoring**, not inbound data refinery.
- The "monetization gateway" / X402 angle — Zaher measures AI visibility, doesn't yet monetize agent access (Agentic Shopping module implies future).
