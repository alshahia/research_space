## What this matrix says at a glance

1. **Zaher.AI is alone in the Arabic/MENA bucket** — first mover, defensible for 18–24 months
2. **The English horizontal SaaS market is saturated** — 13 paid vendors + ~15 OSS, all in the same 6–9 engines
3. **The OSS substrate is mature enough to build an MVP in 4–6 months** without writing prompt-orchestration from scratch
4. **The Agentic Shopping angle is being chased by 5+ vendors** (Zaher, Profound, Goodie, Evertune, Botify) — no clear winner yet
5. **The cheapest paid entry in the matrix is Zaher.AI at $7.99/mo** — a deliberate price wedge for the MENA SMB market
6. **The most defensible moat in the matrix is Zaher's Arabic dialect handling** — no other vendor (paid or OSS) supports Arabic native
7. **MCP server presence is now table stakes** for new entrants — 4 of the 23 listed (Peec, Goodie, Semrush, plus 3 OSS) ship one; Zaher does not
