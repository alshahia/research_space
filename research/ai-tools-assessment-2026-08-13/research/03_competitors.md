Two adjacent markets, one funnel apart. The service sells the transformation; the SaaS sells the measurement. If you later productize (05), the measurement layer is where the SaaS moat is.

## 4. What the comparison says — five things to do differently

1. **Compete on the guarantee, not the price.** No freelancer guarantees 5 hrs/week or refunds; no SaaS can promise time back (they only measure). The guarantee is category-unique for a service.
2. **Compete on the deliverable, not the hours.** A 9-slide stupid-simple quantified report beats a 40-page maturity deck and a 2-page gig PDF. "A confused mind doesn't buy."
3. **Compete on the funnel, not the audit.** The audit's job is the upsell menu; freelancers and agencies sell gigs, you sell a ladder (audit → project → retainer → SaaS).
4. **Compete on local trust, not reach.** Meetups, office hours, referral partners — every SaaS and marketplace is reach-at-scale; none can be "the AI guy in your town" (or "for your industry").
5. **Position against the category's own vocabulary.** Buyers already search "AI readiness assessment" and "AI audit" (thanks to the enterprise frameworks and free SaaS tools). Use their words: your landing page and meetup titles should say "AI audit / AI readiness assessment for [niche]" — you inherit the search intent they built.

## 6. Pricing band benchmark (what buyers are comparing you against)

```
$25–$500  Fiverr/Upwork AI-audit gigs            ← the price floor (and the quality floor)
$999      This playbook's assessment             ← the value anchor
$1.2–2K/mo AI Concierge retainer                  ← premium service tier
$7.99–$800/mo GEO/AEO SaaS (Zaher → Evertune)     ← software alternative
$25K+     Enterprise AI maturity engagements      ← the prestige ceiling
```

**Benchmark takeaway:** at $999 you sit above freelancers (defend with guarantee + deliverable) and far below consultancies (defend with SMB focus + speed). At $1.2–2K/mo the concierge out-prices all consumer coaching and under-prices all agency retainers — the "done-with-you" premium slot is unoccupied at scale.
