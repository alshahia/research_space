# Idea 3 — Zero-Capital Client Acquisition (7 channels, no audience, no money)

> Source: Greg Isenberg (with Corey Ganon), "The $1,000/hour Solo AI business (Full Course)"
> Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dhbcVxYhWaQ
> Transcript timestamps: 35:57 – 49:18

## One-line pitch
Seven client-acquisition methods for the AI assessment/concierge business that require zero capital and zero audience — all manual, all low-competition, because 99% of people won't do them and the rest quit after 3 days.

## The master insight
"These are all things 99% of people will not do. The competition is so low, and the people who do them will do them for 3 days and then quit. Hang in there for 7 days and you're in the top 0.1%." The moat of this business isn't the service — it's the willingness to do uncomfortable outreach.

## The 7 channels

### 1. Local AI-for-business meetup (slow, compounding — "SEO-like")
- Partner with a coworking space: "I'll bring 30 local business owners into your space — can I have your conference room for an hour for free?"
- You organize it → you're automatically the expert
- Format: 10–15 min networking → 20-min talk on **how to use Claude** (simple enough to follow, advanced enough to wow) → work the room
- Collect contact info at the door; follow up within 24 hrs offering the assessment
- Clients come from meetups #3–6, not #1 — treat it as a monthly event brand where you're the leader (SEO framing: month 1 nothing, months 5–16 compounding)

### 2. Door knocking (fastest, best sales education)
- "The single best way to learn sales. Period." Need a client today? Knock a door. This week? Knock a door
- Listener data point from his DMs: 30 local service businesses → asked for the owner/manager → 5 meetings → **2 clients**
- He's knocked hundreds/thousands of doors; the people who do this "are built so different"

### 3. LinkedIn DMs (probe, never pitch)
- Target *local* business owners (not random prospects). Example hook: "Hey Joe, my name's Corey, I probably live down the street from you. Very curious how you're using AI in your business — would love to give you free feedback on ways you could implement it"
- **Message 1 is never a pitch** — pitching in message one is "the quickest way to never get any success from cold DMs"
- Video/voice/Loom works: 30-second phone-video yap beats text

### 4. Free mini-audits to your own network (fastest first client)
- Everyone knows business owners — gym, church, other parents, friends
- Text: "I'm getting into the AI services world. I'd love to spend 15 minutes with you, completely free, showing you one way AI could benefit your business. Open?"
- The mini-audit is the lead magnet: prescribe **one** tool; the full assessment is the upsell
- **Worst-case framing** (the conversion trick): "Worst case you learn one or two tools you never heard of. Best case you have a partner for all this AI stuff that's been on your to-do list." Nobody can say no to "at worst you learn something"

### 5. Agency / referral partners (sustained, low-effort)
- Insurance agents, accountants, marketing agencies, coaches, consultants — all have clients who ask them AI questions they can't answer, and referring badly makes *them* look bad
- Pitch: "I'm in the AI space — I'll happily help any of your clients with AI questions, and I'll send you a referral fee for anyone I work with"
- **Follow up every 2–3 weeks** ("Any clients ask about AI lately? Happy to meet anytime") — one outreach attempt gets you nothing; sustained cadence gets you referrals
- Upgrade: co-branded workshop ("How to use AI to optimize your finances — brought to you by Corey and your tax firm") → they stay top-of-mind with their clients, you get their customer base

### 6. AI office hours at coworking spaces (the pro combo play)
- Pitch the space: "I'll come in once a week for an hour and be a free AI resource for your tenants — they can just walk up and ask me anything"
- Win-win-win: the space gets a value-add for tenants; tenants get a free AI expert; you plug directly into your ICP
- **Dennis's execution** (his community member): reached out to 10 spaces → 2 yeses; door-knocked local businesses/restaurants for free gift cards → $175 in cards as the attendance carrot → 11 people at the first session → 2 follow-up calls booked → ~1 client guaranteed

### 7. Build in public (slowest, most compounding)
- Post every win — and a win is smaller than you think: "someone texted me a ChatGPT question and I gave a great answer" is a win
- Early days have no wins → manufacture valuable content for your ICP: **90-day plan of one killer prompt/day** + a carousel, stuck to religiously; real results layer on once clients start texting "I implemented this"

## The combinations (the actual pro moves)
- **Meetup × agency partner:** his first meetup was hosted by a well-connected Charlotte realtor in her office — she invited her clients and fellow realtors ("a big free AI enablement session"); he got a client out of it
- **Office hours × door knocking:** Dennis's gift-card hustle
- **Any of these × assessment → concierge:** the channels feed the trip wire; the trip wire feeds the retainer

## Recommended 30/60/90 launch plan
- **Days 1–30:** 10 free mini-audits to your network (build the skill with real transcripts); door knock 30 businesses in your wedge; stand up one meetup or office-hours slot
- **Days 31–60:** first 3–5 paid assessments; post every win (however small); start the referral-partner follow-up cadence
- **Days 61–90:** 5–10 paid assessments + first upsell closes; LinkedIn DM lane running; office hours at 2 coworking spaces; decide your ONE upsell specialty

## Localization (MENA)
- LinkedIn DMs → **WhatsApp Business DMs** (MENA-native; same probe-don't-pitch rules)
- Meetups → Arabic-first business meetups; partner with co-working spaces (e.g. GrEEK Campus–style venues in Cairo), chambers of commerce, and industry associations
- Referral partners → accountants and real-estate brokers are the highest-signal network in most MENA markets; offer the same referral fee
- Office hours work identically in Cairo/GCC co-working scenes — and the gift-card combo translates to local cafés/restaurants
- Door knocking works everywhere it's socially acceptable; in gated/office-tower markets, swap it for in-person visits to shops/offices with a manager-facing ask
