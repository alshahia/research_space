# Idea 2 — The AI Concierge Retainer ($1,000/hour recurring)

> Source: Greg Isenberg (with Corey Ganon), "The $1,000/hour Solo AI business (Full Course)"
> Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dhbcVxYhWaQ
> Transcript timestamps: 49:18 – 58:43

## One-line pitch
A "done-with-you" monthly retainer: two 45-minute working sessions where you teach the business owner to build Claude skills on their own workflows (AOA), plus on-demand Voxer access — priced so the billable hours alone are worth ~$1,000/hour.

## Why this exists
The assessment uncovers 3–7 quick wins and a backlog of "major projects" (skills, agents, knowledge bases). The owner has no idea how to build a Claude skill — that 20–30-minute build is a wall for them and a two-minute task for you. Instead of selling one-off builds, you sell **yourself as their in-house AI department, twice a month, forever**. The assessment is the discovery mechanism; the concierge is the recurring revenue engine.

## The offer anatomy
- **2 × 45-min Zoom working sessions/month** — client shares their screen; you build Claude skills together and teach Claude Co-work. That's literally it
- **Unlimited Voxer access, 12-business-hour response** — the perceived-value weapon: sounds like 24/7 support, reality is ~1 message per client per 2.5 months (3 of 5 clients messaged once each). "Virtually zero workload, very high perceived value"
- **First call = foundation, not build:** a Claude plugin walks the client through guided onboarding — connect tools, context files, global instructions, scheduled tasks, what a skill does. Every call after is 10× more effective
- **Every session after = AOA on one workflow:** **A**udit ("show me how you do it today") → **O**ptimize ("13 steps → 7") → **A**utomate ("turn it into a Claude skill"). Rinse, repeat, for the life of the engagement

## The economics (from the episode)
| Client # | Price/mo | Cumulative MRR |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $1,200 | $1,200 |
| 2–3 | $1,500 each | $4,200 |
| 4 | $1,800 | $6,000 |
| 5 | $2,000 | $8,000 |

- **$8K MRR within 10 days of launching the offer** — 5 of the first 6 people he told bought; he raised the price after every yes and they kept saying yes
- Effective rate: $1,500/mo ÷ 1.5 billable hrs = **$1,000/hour** (plus Notion upkeep, minus nothing)
- Cap: 6 clients, legitimately. Every sales call: "I'm taking one more client and I have two other calls this week — if you say no today and one says yes, I'm not available until someone churns." Scarcity must be real

## What makes it feel premium (the retention mechanics)
1. **Pre-engagement Google Form** (mini-audit) with the killer question: *"90 days from now, what would make this engagement feel like a win?"* → 4 of 5 clients hit their stated 90-day win by day ~60
2. **The win reminder:** when you exceed their stated goal, remind them in writing, then upsell again — "there's always an upsell to the upsell to the upsell"
3. **Notion one-pager hub:** the entire engagement + an inventory of everything accomplished, updated and emailed after every call. It's the thing staring at them that says "keep paying $1,800"
4. **Landing-page scarcity (Greg's tip):** 4 spots @ $800/mo → then $1,200 → then $1,600. People buy on urgency; AI's built-in urgency ("everyone's worried about falling behind") still needs a trigger to make *you* the hire

## Where it plugs into the funnel
- Natural upsell from the assessment's "what comes after quick wins" slide (major projects = your session backlog)
- Easier than retainers on other services ("$3K/mo for 2 knowledge systems" also works — he suggests retainers around anything once trust exists)
- Up-market forever: when clients churn, package "the $10K/hour experience." There is always a more luxury tier for a specific ICP
- The recommended path for most people: **do the assessment + pick ONE upsell.** He recommends the concierge; others specialize in knowledge systems or skills only

## Fit / anti-fit
- **Fits if:** you actually know Claude skills/Co-work cold (the first-call onboarding must feel effortless); you like 1:1 coaching; you want recurring revenue without building software
- **Anti-fits:** if you hate teaching, or if you want leverage — the cap of 6 is the ceiling by design (the escape hatch is productizing, i.e. the Zaher.AI arc)

## Localization (MENA)
- Price tiers in local currency: e.g. EGP 25–60K/mo for premium SMBs, USD for GCC; keep the cap/scarcity mechanics
- Voxer → WhatsApp Business (MENA-native; same "unlimited access, near-zero load" dynamic)
- First-call onboarding stays identical — Claude handles Arabic; the skills you build with them are language-neutral workflow skills
