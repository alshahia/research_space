# NotebookLM / Gemini Notebook — Competitor & Similar-Product Findings

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Scope:** Commercial products only. Open-source projects (AnythingLLM, RAGFlow, Open Notebook, Open-NotebookLM, SurfSense, LM Studio, Zotero, Logseq, Joplin) are a separate agent's workstream — listed only in the scope note at the end.
**Method:** Web research (Exa + Firecrawl), ~28 queries, official pricing pages + help centers + reviews. Per-claim confidence logged in [PROGRESS.md](./PROGRESS.md).

> **Naming note (critical):** Google renamed **NotebookLM → "Gemini Notebook"** in July 2026. Same product; old notebooks preserved; URLs redirect (`notebooklm.google.com` → `notebook.google.com`). Sources: [notebooklm.google/plans](https://notebooklm.google/plans), [notebookcheck.net](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Your-second-brain-Obsidian-Notion-and-who-lets-the-AI-in.1351962.0.html). Most third-party guides still say "NotebookLM".

## 2. Per-product profiles

### 2.1 General AI assistants (attachments + project workspaces)

**Gemini Notebook (Google, baseline product).** Uploaded-source research assistant built on Gemini. 100 notebooks free, 50 sources each (500k words / 200MB per source), 50 chats/day, 3 audio overviews/day. Outputs: grounded chat, study guides, reports, flashcards, quizzes, mind maps, infographics, slide decks, audio + video overviews, Deep Research (10/mo free). Plus (Google AI Plus ~$20/mo, 2TB storage): 100 sources, 6 audio/day. Pro: 300 sources, 20 audio/day. Ultra: 500–600 sources, up to 200 audio/day. Strengths vs rivals: free tier, source grounding, best-in-class audio/video overviews, Google ecosystem fit. Weaknesses: source-level (not page-level) citations, 50-source cap on free, Google account required, no API, no offline, no team collaboration, weak at literature discovery (uploads only). Sources: [notebooklm.google/plans](https://notebooklm.google/plans), [support.google.com limits](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16213268), [FAQ](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16269187), [gemini.google/subscriptions](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/).

**ChatGPT (OpenAI).** Not a notebook, but the default generalist: file uploads in-chat, Projects (grouped chats + reference files + custom instructions; file caps 5/25/40 by tier; shared projects for Business/Edu), Canvas (writing/code workspace with versioning, PDF/MD/DOCX export), custom GPTs, Deep Research, web search with citations. No audio-overview feature. Plus $20/mo is the competitive slot; free tier works for light use. Strengths: model quality, tool breadth, familiarity. Weaknesses vs NotebookLM: smaller source caps per project (40 max), weaker persistent grounding semantics (retrieval over project files exists but citations are looser), no podcast output, file upload count limits. Sources: [help.openai.com Projects](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10169521-projects-in-chatgpt), [Canvas help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9930697-what-is-the-canvas-featue-in-chatgpt-and-how-do-i-use-it), [openai.com/chatgpt/pricing](https://openai.com/chatgpt/pricing/), [learn.chatgpt.com](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/projects).

**Claude (Anthropic).** Claude.ai with Projects: per-project chat histories + knowledge base (upload 30MB files, unlimited count; auto-RAG up to 10?� context when knowledge exceeds the window). Artifacts give interactive documents/code side-by-side. Research feature for long multi-source reports. Pro $20/mo, Max $100–200/mo. Strengths: strongest raw reasoning, clean grounding from uploaded files, generous project scale via RAG. Weaknesses vs NotebookLM: no audio overviews, no study-guide/flashcard suite, no deep academic tooling. Sources: [claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing), [Claude Projects help](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9519177-how-can-i-create-and-manage-projects), [RAG for projects](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/11473015-retrieval-), [Artifacts help](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9487310-what-are-artifacts-and-how-do-i-use-them), [Upload files](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8241126-upload-files-to-claude).

**Perplexity (Spaces, Pages, Comet).** Search-first research engine; every answer carries inline citations. Spaces = persistent project workspaces bundling threads, uploaded files (50/project Pro; 500 Enterprise Pro/Max; 5,000 Enterprise Max; 50MB files), custom instructions, collaborators. Pages = turn a thread into a shareable formatted article. Deep Research = long multi-source cited reports. Comet = free agentic Chromium browser with sidecar assistant. Pro $20/mo ($200/yr); Max $200/mo; Enterprise Pro $40/user; Enterprise Max $325/user. Strengths vs NotebookLM: live-web grounding, click-to-audit citations, connectors, 400+ app integrations, multi-model access. Weaknesses: not a long-term knowledge base (thread-files auto-delete after 7 days outside Spaces), weaker document ingestion than NotebookLM, no audio overviews, no study tools. Sources: [perplexity.ai/hub/pricing](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/pricing), [Spaces help](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10352961-what-are-spaces), [Comet](https://www.perplexity.ai/comet), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/02/perplexitys-comet-ai-browser-now-free-max-users-get-new-background-assistant/), [The AI Rankings review](https://theairankings.com/perplexity/perplexity/), [ToolChase review](https://toolchase.com/blog/perplexity-ai-review/).

**Microsoft Copilot family.** Three relevant surfaces: (1) free consumer Copilot (chat + file uploads, capped credits, peak-time limits); (2) Copilot Pro $20/mo (requires M365 Personal/Family; priority access, deep research, in-app Copilot across Word/Excel/PPT/Outlook); (3) Microsoft 365 Copilot $30/user/mo (tenant grounding across Graph: files, email, meetings; SharePoint agents; promo pricing $18–25 on Business plans). Copilot Studio ($200/25k Copilot Credits pack or pay-as-you-go) is the enterprise agent builder: agents answer from uploaded documents (up to 500 files, 512MB each — PDF, Word, Excel, PPT, MD, HTML, CSV, JSON, LaTeX, EPUB…), SharePoint/OneDrive/Dataverse, Azure AI Search. Strengths vs NotebookLM: enterprise data access, IT governance, in-app productivity. Weaknesses: consumer tier is throttled, no notebook paradigm, no audio overviews, pricing complexity. Sources: [microsoft.com Copilot pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing), [CDW Copilot Pro vs Copilot](https://www.cdw.com/content/cdw/en/articles/software/microsoft-copilot-pro-vs-copilot.html), [Copilot Studio pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing/copilot-studio), [Learn: upload files as knowledge source](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/knowledge-add-file-upload), [Learn: licensing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/billing-licensing).

**Google Gemini app (Deep Research, Files).** Adjacent Google product: chat with files in the Gemini app and Deep Research (multi-step cited reports). Shares the Google AI Plus/Pro/Ultra tiers with Gemini Notebook. Included for completeness; it competes with NotebookLM on research but is not a notebook. Source: [gemini.google/subscriptions](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/), [support.google.com/googleone](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/16882689).

### 2.2 Academic research tools (the "paper" competitors)

**Elicit.** Literature-workflow specialist: semantic + keyword search over 138M+ papers and 545k clinical trials, structured extraction tables (PICO, sample sizes, arms), systematic-review workflow (screen up to 5,000 papers on Pro, 20,000 Scale, 40,000 Enterprise) with PRISMA-auditable decisions, reports with sentence-level citations, CSV/RIS/BIB export, Zotero import, BYOData uploads. Free tier limited; Pro $49/user/mo annual ($588/yr); Scale $169/user/mo. Strengths vs NotebookLM: paper discovery (NotebookLM can't find papers), extraction rigor, per-claim sentence citations. Weaknesses: no audio, no study-guide output, no arbitrary-document workspace (paper-focused), steeper learning curve. Sources: [elicit.com/pricing](https://elicit.com/pricing), [Systematic Reviews help](https://support.elicit.com/en/articles/14759154-systematic-reviews-in-elicit), [elicit.com/solutions/systematic-review](https://elicit.com/solutions/systematic-review).

**Consensus.** Evidence-synthesis engine: searches 200–220M+ peer-reviewed papers, returns a consensus answer + Consensus Meter showing direction of the literature; Pro Analysis reads 20+ papers, Deep Reviews screen up to 1,000 papers; study snapshots, evidence tables, upload-your-own-papers, Zotero/Paperpile/Endnote export, clinical mode. Free tier; Pro $20/mo or $144/yr; Deep $65/mo ($540/yr). Strengths vs NotebookLM: real-paper grounding, no uploads needed, evidence-typed citations. Weaknesses: peer-reviewed corpus only (no mixed personal docs), no audio, no deep multi-format study outputs. Sources: [consensus.app/pricing](https://consensus.app/pricing), [help: Premium features](https://help.consensus.app/en/articles/10280275-what-do-you-get-with-consensus-premium), [help: plans](https://help.consensus.app/en/articles/10087865-subscription-plans), [features/trial](https://consensus.app/home/features/start-trial/).

**SciSpace (formerly Typeset).** Paper reading + chat: chat-with-PDF, paper explanations, literature review, AI writer, paraphraser, citation generator, data extraction, AI detector, agents; searches 270M+ papers. Free tier; Premium $20/mo ($12 annual); Advanced $90/mo ($70 annual); Max $200/mo ($160 annual). Strengths vs NotebookLM: academic search built in, citation tooling. Weaknesses: paper-centric, credit-metered agents, no audio, no mixed-format workspace. Source: [scispace.com/pricing](https://scispace.com/pricing).

**Julius AI.** Data-analysis assistant (not a citation tool): upload CSV/Excel/JSON/SPSS/PDF, connect Google Sheets/Drive/OneDrive/Snowflake/BigQuery, natural-language stats, visualizations, slides. Free tier; Plus $20/mo ($16 annual); Pro $45/mo. Strengths: real data science in chat. Weaknesses: no document-grounded citations, no audio. Sources: [julius.ai/pricing](https://julius.ai/pricing), [julius.ai/docs](https://julius.ai/docs/get-started/files).

### 2.3 Document Q&A / RAG chat tools

**ChatPDF.** Fast single/multi-PDF chat: free 2–3 PDFs/day (10MB, 120pp), Plus $5/mo (50/day, 32MB, 2,000pp), page-level clickable citations, side-by-side reading, no-account start, multi-file folders, desktop + mobile apps. Strengths: zero-friction, cheap, precise page citations. Weaknesses: PDF-centric, no audio, no study tools, thin feature set vs NotebookLM. Sources: [chatpdf.com](https://www.chatpdf.com/), [otio.ai pricing breakdown](https://otio.ai/blog/how-does-chatpdf-work).

**DocumindAI.** Business document review: PDFs/Word/TXT for contracts, policies, invoices, board materials; outputs decision-ready summaries, extracted risks/obligations/deadlines, source-backed Q&A with inspectable evidence. Pricing not published on the analyzed page (gap). Distinct from the IDP platform documind.cloud (intelligent document processing for invoice/contract automation). Sources: [documindai.com/analyze](https://documindai.com/analyze), [documind.cloud](https://documind.cloud/).

**Adobe Acrobat — Liquid Mode + Acrobat AI Assistant.** Liquid Mode (Sensei-powered, mobile-only) reflows PDFs for reading; Acrobat AI Assistant adds summaries, outlines, key points and Q&A with **attribution links that jump to the exact PDF passage** (the page-level citation NotebookLM lacks). Priced within Acrobat Standard $12.99/mo / Pro $19.99/mo; AI Assistant add-on ~$5/mo (medium confidence — bundling changed). Weaknesses: single-PDF focus, mobile-only reflow, no audio. Sources: [Adobe Liquid Mode](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/hub/what-is-adobe-liquid-mode.html), [Acrobat AI Assistant](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/generative-ai-pdf.html), [devnet docs](https://www.adobe.com/devnet-docs/acrobat/android/en/lmode.html).

### 2.4 Audio-overview competitors

**ElevenLabs GenFM.** The direct competitor to NotebookLM's Audio Overviews: paste URLs/text or upload files, get podcast-style audio discussions with natural multi-host voices; runs on ElevenReader (iOS/Android) and web; now folded into ElevenLabs Studio 3.0 (full audio/video editor, 30+ languages). Credit-based pricing: Free 10k credits/mo; Starter $5; Creator ~$22/mo (121k credits). Strengths: better voice quality, more languages, deeper audio editing. Weaknesses: no research/Q&A layer — audio is the whole product. Sources: [elevenlabs.io/studio](https://elevenlabs.io/genfm) (redirect), [elevenlabs.io/pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing), [GenFM on ElevenReader blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/genfm-on-elevenreader), [flexprice breakdown](https://flexprice.io/blog/elevenlabs-pricing-breakdown).

### 2.5 Enterprise knowledge / agent platforms

**Notion AI (Q&A, Research Mode, Agent).** Q&A answers from workspace pages with page citations and refuses when nothing is found; Research Mode drafts detailed docs from workspace + connectors + web; AI meeting notes; database autofill; Notion Agent ($10/1,000 credits) for autonomous multi-step tasks; Enterprise Search across connected apps (Slack, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Gmail, GitHub, Jira, Linear…). Pricing: Free tier; Plus $10/user/mo; Business $20/user/mo with AI included (since May 2025 repricing). Strengths vs NotebookLM: workspace-native, team collaboration, connector breadth. Weaknesses: no audio, Q&A misses database logic, weaker at standalone research corpora. Sources: [notion.com/pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing), [Notion AI for Work](https://www.notion.com/blog/notion-ai-for-work), [Q&A guide](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/get-answers-about-content-faster-with-q-and-a), [Q&A mechanics](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/understanding-how-q-and-a-finds-answers-can-help-you-get-better-results).

**Dust.** Multiplayer AI workspace for teams: build custom assistants/agents on GPT/Claude/Gemini/Mistral with workspace knowledge, connectors, MCP servers, actions, Frames; SOC 2 Type II. Free (500 lifetime credits); Pro $29/user/mo; Business; Enterprise custom ($0.01/credit programmatic). Strengths: team agent-building, multi-model. Weaknesses: developer-leaning, no consumer notebook UX, no audio. Sources: [dust.tt/home/pricing](https://dust.tt/home/pricing), [dust.tt](https://dust.tt/), [Tooliverse review](https://tooliverse.ai/tools/dust).

**Lindy.** No-code autonomous AI assistants (calendar, email triage, web research tasks). From $49.99/mo per third-party comparison (official pricing not scraped — medium confidence). Strengths: autonomy for repetitive work. Weaknesses: not a document-grounded research notebook; reliability varies on long tasks. Sources: [usecarly.com Dust/Lindy comparison](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/dust-alternatives/), [vellum.ai Lindy alternatives](https://www.vellum.ai/blog/best-lindy-ai-alternatives-2026).

**Fixie.** AI agent-building platform for business workflows (document + system grounding). Low confidence: no current official pricing or acquisition info found in this pass; verify before use. Source: [LinkedIn AI-agents overview](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-agents-revolution-what-you-need-know-2025-alexandru-stan-zsbbf).

### 2.6 Education

Pricing not published on the site (gap). Note: a different, unrelated product — fermat.app — is an AI fashion-design tool. Sources: [fermat-app.space](https://www.fermat-app.space/), [fermat.app](https://www.fermat.app/) (disambiguation).

## 5. Key takeaways

1. **No direct full-stack competitor exists.** NotebookLM/Gemini Notebook is uniquely the combination of: free source-grounded chat + audio/video overviews + study tools (flashcards/quizzes/mind maps) + mixed-format ingestion (PDF→YouTube→audio).
2. **The audio moat is contested by exactly one company:** ElevenLabs GenFM (voice quality, languages) — and Google itself with Video Overviews. PodGenie/BeFreed/Wondercraft/Fermat nibble at the edges.
3. **Citations are the battleground.** NotebookLM gives source-level citations; competitors (ChatPDF, Adobe, Sidenote, Elicit, Consensus, Ponder) differentiate on page/sentence-level traceability — a documented NotebookLM weakness (no page-level jumps; quality regression reports since Gemini 3.1 Pro).
4. **The $20/mo cluster dominates:** ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Perplexity Pro, Copilot Pro, Gemini Notebook Plus/Pro, SciSpace Premium, Consensus Pro — all ~$20. Free tiers vary wildly in usefulness.
5. **Academic tools win on discovery, not synthesis:** Elicit/Consensus/SciSpace/Scite handle finding and evidence-checking; NotebookLM remains the best synthesis surface for a source set you already control.
6. **Enterprise grounding is Microsoft's lane** (Copilot Studio + tenant data), while team knowledge is Notion's (Q&A over workspace + connectors).
7. **Pricing volatility is real:** Elicit ($10→$49 tiers), Consensus ($9.99→$20), SciSpace, M365 Copilot promos all shifted in the last 12 months — treat all prices as point-in-time (2026-08-10).
