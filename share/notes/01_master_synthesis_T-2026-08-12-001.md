## The headline finding

**Kotobee is a publishing/delivery toolchain with a storefront bolted on — not a marketplace.** The research is unambiguous on this: the company's 300,000+ customers are mostly institutions (Harvard, Yale, Pearson, Oracle, Aramco, MENA governments); consumer retail is a secondary use case; the storefront (books.kotobee.com) has no public traffic stats and no charts. So "what sells on Kotobee" has two honest answers: (a) what Kotobee *structurally supports* — interactive educational/children's/training content where its features (quizzes, DRM, SCORM/LMS, Arabic RTL) are a genuine moat, and (b) what *sells globally* — fiction, which belongs on Amazon/Kobo with Kotobee as the production tool.

**The genre ranking in the canonical file blends those two lenses (60/40).** Ranks 1–4 and 8–10 are Kotobee-native lanes; ranks 5–7 are Amazon-export lanes. If the user is Arabic-speaking (likely — timezone +03:00), the Arabic lane (children's/educational/self-development/faith) is where Kotobee beats every competitor: no other toolchain is RTL-first, Arabic-localized, and free.

## What I think the user should know before Phase 2

1. **Start at $0.** Free Author license + free Kotobee Books publishing + own Stripe/PayPal. The $150–$300 paid tiers buy branding/AI/SCORM — not needed for book #1.
2. **100% royalties is a promo, not a contract.** Plan economics at 70% and treat 100% as upside.
3. **Never single-channel.** Publish on Kotobee Books (100% royalties, interactive) AND export EPUB to KDP/Apple/Google (reach). The 90%-sell-<100-copies stat applies everywhere; Kotobee alone will not fix it.
4. **The AI angle is platform-endorsed.** Kotobee Author ships AI text/image/question/TTS generators; the blog promotes AI writing tools. An LLM/agent-assisted workflow is consistent with the platform — but human-directed, fact-checked, and disclosed (KDP requires AI disclosure; Kotobee Books ToS AI clause must be read at signup).
5. **K-lytics is live at k-lytics.com** (the strategy angle checked the no-hyphen squatter domain — resolved in merge). It is the best paid tool for Kindle niche selection; WordsRated is the best free one.
6. **The institutional lane is Kotobee's biggest unique lever:** package a book as a course (LTI/SCORM/xAPI) and sell site licenses — that's how Kotobee case-study authors actually made money (MedEducation +10% margin, Diction Police tripled sales).

## Bias check (what could be wrong in this research)

- **Arabic-market demand is qualitative.** No free quantitative source exists; I'm not overstating it, but it is inference from ecosystem evidence.
- **"Low competition inside the storefront" is inference** (from supply counts + absence of charts), not a measurement of demand.
- **Global figures carry old vintages** (2017–2023 underlying surveys even on 2026-refreshed pages). Genre dominance (romance/thriller/self-help/children's) is stable over 3+ years, so ranking survives; exact percentages may not.
- **Supply counts ≠ sales.** Educational has 3,589 titles listed; that measures author supply, not buyer demand. Flagged everywhere.
