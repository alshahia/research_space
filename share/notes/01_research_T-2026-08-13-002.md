## 1. Working definition (from Angle A)

A top-tier AI research product in 2026 is an agentic system that, given a topic from a non-expert user, autonomously:

1. Decomposes the topic into a multi-step, editable research plan.
2. Retrieves from web + academic + code + data + news + books at the primary-source level (not just abstracts).
3. Reasons about evidence, weighs contradictions, surfaces uncertainty.
4. Synthesizes a citation-rich, multi-format output (report + tables + sources + audio/mind-map).
5. Persists state across turns; supports follow-ups and source-attached mid-run redirection.
6. Honors a calibration contract: refuses cleanly when it can't answer; names the gap when it can't access a source.

This separates a research product from a chatbot: the former plans, browses, retrieves, executes, cites, persists; the latter answers one prompt from parametric memory.

## 2. Differentiating axes (the things that matter most)

From Angle A §3 (with cross-validation from Angle B user-complaint clusters and Angle D synthesis patterns):

1. **Citation accuracy × citation density** — the "trust compound." Perplexity DR 90.24% accuracy; Gemini DR 111.21 effective citations (DeepResearch Bench). Inline citations linked to specific passages, not URL-and-hope.
2. **Agentic depth × effort control** — user-controllable thinking budget. Anthropic effort parameter; OpenAI DR 5–30 min; Gemini DR plan-and-approve-before-run; the Adaptive orchestration already in `agents_manager/SKILL.md` is the same pattern at the orchestrator layer.
3. **Multi-agent orchestration × self-critique** — the verifier loop. Deep Research Agents beat LLMs-with-search 3–10× on effective citations (Anthropic 2025-06). The orchestration + verification loop is where gains live, not in retrieval alone.

Secondary axes (do not ship without, but do not lead with):
- Multi-modal live grounding (URL/PDF/image ingest).
- Calibrated uncertainty / honest refusal when evidence is thin.

## 5. Synthesis architecture (from Angle D, summarized)

Two patterns research_space should treat as load-bearing:

- **Anthropic Research** (Opus lead + Sonnet subagents + CitationAgent) — for breadth-first queries. Maps almost 1:1 to the existing `agents_manager/` multi-agent model (master + 10 specialists + soft-wall boundaries). The plan-memory artifact can be reused.
- **Magentic-One** (Orchestrator + WebSurfer + FileSurfer + Coder + ComputerTerminal) — for open-ended task solving; not a research product on its own.

Implementation roadmap (4 stages):

1. **v1 — single-agent tool loop** (4–6 weeks). Reuse `agents_manager/coder/`. Add `lib/sources/{web,arxiv,pubmed}.ts`. Add `lib/citations.ts` (`[S1]…[Sn]` markers + abstention). Add `lib/synthesis.ts`.
2. **v2 — verifier** (2–3 weeks). CitationAgent stage. FActScore atomic decomposition. Source-confidence scoring.
3. **v3 — multi-agent orchestrator** (3–4 weeks). LeadResearcher + parallel subagents. Reuse `share/notes/` bus; add per-user `users/<id>/runs/<runId>/` namespace.
4. **v4 — contradiction + audit** (2–3 weeks). Contradiction-detection prompt. Per-user audit-trail renderer.

Total: ~12–20 weeks one developer, ~6–10 if reusing agents_manager primitives aggressively.
