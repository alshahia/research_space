### Top license traps (avoid in any closed-source build)

1. **Mapbox BSL/TOS** — `flutter-mapbox-gl`, `mapbox_maps_flutter`, `mapbox-maps-ios`. Mapbox SDK moved to Business Source License 2020; TOS forbids using it in a "Mapbox-Competing Product". **Use MapLibre instead** — same API surface, BSD-2-Clause [Angle D.5].
2. **GPL-3.0 samples** — `SecUSo/privacy-friendly-pedometer` [S44], `cfilipov/MuscleBook` [S45]. Linking a GPL library into an iOS app is argued (in court) to require source disclosure of the entire app. **Don't link GPL in shipping binary.**
3. **Unlicensed repos** — `azuzunaga/lucha` [S47], `etrinidad132/Esforza` [S48], `ankur874/GPS-Running-Tracker` [S46], `Borisserz/WorkoutTracker` [S49], `Cairo-Squad/EvolveFit` [S50], `EvanBacon/apple-health` [S51]. All-rights-reserved by default in non-US jurisdictions; **reject for any vendor decision**.

### Vendor-vs-build verdict (per pillar)

- GPS run tracking → **VENDOR** (Apple `CoreLocation`/`CoreMotion` direct; or Transistorsoft Flutter/RN plugin)
- Step counting → **VENDOR** (`CMPedometer` direct or `pedometer` Flutter package [S57])
- Map rendering → **VENDOR** (MapLibre Native [S32] or Apple MapKit)
- GPX/TCX export → **VENDOR** (CoreGPX [S30] Swift or `gpx` Flutter [S58])
- Apple Health write-back → **VENDOR** (`HKWorkoutBuilder` direct or `health` Flutter [S31])
- Health Connect write-back → **VENDOR** (AndroidX `health-connect-client`)
- Training plans → **BUILD** (no good OSS)
- Social backend → **BUILD** (or **CloudKit** for iOS-only MVP — free, zero backend code)
- Analytics + retention → **VENDOR** (PostHog [S70] cloud or Sentry [S68])

### "Start Monday" recommendation

**iOS-only path (chosen)**: SwiftUI app + CoreGPX (MIT) + Apple MapKit (free) + CoreLocation/CoreMotion direct + HealthKit + **CloudKit** (zero-backend social/records store). Study OpenTracks [S29] for sensor-fusion lessons, don't fork.

**Cross-platform fallback (if Android at v1)**: Flutter + `health` (MIT) [S31] + `flutter_background_geolocation` (Apache-2.0 SDK) [S33] + `gpx` (Apache-2.0) [S58] + MapLibre (`flutter_maplibre_gl` BSD-2-Clause) [S42]. Closest full Flutter clone: `BenjaminCanape/RunFlutterRun` (MIT) [S34] — borderline stale, clone and rebuild UI.

### Prime issue

**Background GPS run tracking that is accurate enough to be trusted, but light enough on battery that a runner finishes a 10K without reaching for a charger.** Every other feature (gamification, leaderboards, templates, pricing) is downstream of whether the recorded distance, pace, and route are right [Angle E.1].

Acceptance criterion: 5K on iPhone 14 (100% → 91%) — route polyline within 10m of sidewalk, pace ±5 s/km, battery <12% per 60 min.

Why this beats the alternatives:
- **Permission UX** is mechanical (iOS 16+ has standard prompt choreography) [S62].
- **Cold-start-to-first-run latency** is a one-time onboarding problem.
- **Subscription paywall** is commercial, not technical.
- **Apple Watch companion** is v1.5 (parity with Runify's own iPhone-only choice).
- **Battery drain during a long run** is the *other side* of the prime issue.

### Audience focus

**Primary**: "Amir, 28, competitive phone-runner" — software engineer or grad student, runs 3–5x/week, has iPhone + Apple Watch, resents $11.99/mo Strava, wants a $4.99/mo visible-rank loop.

**Secondary**: "Layla, 32, streak-proud health-curious" — working mother, runs 2–3K 3–4x/week, values streak counter and weekly digest over leaderboard.

**Excluded**: "Coach Tom, the serious distance runner training for a sub-3 marathon" — already pays Strava + Garmin + Stryd. **Build away from him**: no Stryd pairing, no FTP testing, no VO2max, no lactate thresholds in v1.

Geography: **US App Store, English-only, paid via StoreKit 2** [S1][S67]. Expand to UK/CA/AU in 3 months. Defer MENA / Arabic localisation to v1.2.

## 5. Strategic gaps and white-space opportunities

### Top 5 strategic moves (synthesized across angles)

1. **Ship Apple Watch native first** — Runify loses 10 of 13 competitors here; an Apple-Watch-first competitor (Runify-Pro vs Runify-lite framing) is the most defensible single move [Angle C.4]. Pair with `HKLiveWorkoutBuilder` for wrist-route rendering.

2. **Build for the rank-decay-loyalist, not the Strava-loyalist** — Strava's segments are an emergent-competition mechanism (no ceiling); Runify's rank-decay is an individual-axis (caps at Iridescent). A new entrant that combines Strava's "no ceiling" with Runify's "lose rank if inactive" owns both: weekly league reset (Bronze → Silver → Gold based on the week's distance) where the visible leaderboard is *only* your bracket — "Clash of Clans recruitment for runners" [Angle C.7].

3. **Pivot to designed recap-card-as-CAC** — Instagram Stories export is Runify's unique share primitive [S1][S12]. A competitor that ships 20+ designed templates (Runify has 3 free + 2 Pro) and lets users co-create custom templates is the *cheapest* CAC channel because the user is the marketing channel.

4. **Apple Health-as-the-friend-graph** — instead of building your own social network, leverage iPhone Contacts to find which address-book entries are also users. No competitor does this. Strava uses email + Strava IDs; Pacer uses email. The "your friends already have phones, learn to share with them" angle is unclaimed [Angle C.7]. **Risk**: App Store privacy-policy review will be severe (see trust-cliff #5 above).

5. **Own the privacy-trust narrative** — Runify declares **Contacts** but doesn't market it; declares health/fitness/location extensively; carrd.co policy has no GDPR/CCPA/retention language [S2 vs S4]. A competitor that ships with explicit GDPR Art. 15 (Right to Access) one-tap data export, no full-contacts read, and an EU-region backend is the only credible "privacy-first run tracker" in the segment. Maps onto Angle D vendor verdict: PostHog Cloud (EU region) [S70] + Sentry [S68] = compliant-by-default stack.

### What to NOT copy

1. **Trainer-led video workouts** (Apple Fitness+/NRC) — Runify/competitor should NOT become a media company; 12-month content pipeline.
2. **Personalized AI coach** (Google Health Coach on Gemini) — 2-year, $10M+ project. Runify has no AI training data.
3. **Strava-style live tracking maps for friends** — privacy liability + server cost; market has moved to designed-share graphics (which Runify already does).

### Cheap wins (high leverage, low cost) Runify could copy

1. Strava-style segments on city paths — strongest single social hook.
2. Apple Health workout rename-on-import — one-line fix for user's #1 review punchline.
3. Couch-to-5K + half-marathon training plans — NRC shows this converts beginners.
4. GPX / TCX export — power users stop feeling locked-in.
