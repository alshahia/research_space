## Risks and doubts (16 consolidated: 5 HIGH, 7 MEDIUM, 4 LOW)

- **Severity: high - License traps concentrate where the OSS landscape is richest:** unlicensed MOBA demos, the paid Odin dependency inside EX-GAS, Boss Room NOASSERTION, DOTween's modified-redistribution ban, GPL engines. Mitigation: learn-from-only for anything not MIT/Apache-2.0, verify per-file headers, document every reuse [S99][S100][S104][S106][S121].
- **Severity: high - Unity 6 NavMeshAgent SetDestination pause/jitter** (community-reported regression) hits the feel-critical hero path. Mitigation: retarget-on-change discipline, pathPending guard, manual-locomotion fallback (updatePosition=false + agent.Move or CharacterController.Move) [S21][S87][S86][S22].
- **Severity: high - Scope creep:** a full 5v5/3-lane/item-shop/two-AI-teams MOBA is multi-month; without a hard MVP gate the pipeline stalls. Mitigation: P0-P13 done-when gates; lock MVP (1 hero, 1 lane loop) before P6 [07_BUILD_GUIDE.md].
- **Severity: high - Balance-number drift:** every value is a 2026-08-14 snapshot and LoL/MLBB re-tune every patch (plate gold 160 to 175, Cash Back 6% to 8% to 7.5%). Mitigation: all tunables in ScriptableObjects; numbers are starting points, not contracts [S49][S38].
- **Severity: high - Unity docs version/API drift:** multiple 6000.0 manual URLs 404'd in the angles (docs moved to package manuals). Mitigation: cite package-manual URLs (com.unity.*), pin versions in manifest.json [S2][S4][S10].
- **Severity: medium - Bot believability:** all three references keep bot tech proprietary; only behavior lists are public. Mitigation: implement the documented checklist as the v1 floor; believability is a polish loop [S28][S44][S45].
- **Severity: medium - Wiki contradictions and single-source values:** MLBB lane naming conflicts, bot queue name conflicts, truncated fetches. Mitigation: flag values provisional; prefer official sources where they exist [S58][S59][S25].
- **Severity: medium - Touch ergonomics** (joystick deadzones, drag-aim sensitivity) cannot be validated without a device. Mitigation: settings-driven from phase 12, test on real hardware [S78][S75].
- **Severity: medium - Framework adoption risk:** GAS candidates are young or self-admitted unstable; Unity Behavior is a young official package; adopting a framework pulls in dependencies. Mitigation: GAS spike in milestone 0; keep the AI decision core behind an interface [S106][S107][S108][S18].
- **Severity: medium - Brush/vision rules easy to implement wrong** (who can target whom, reveal timers, the AI perception seam). Mitigation: one explicit rule set (Q11), tests, AI reads the same FoW [S53][S61][S54].
- **Severity: medium - Crowd quality on a shared NavMesh:** 10 heroes plus minion swarms retargeting constantly degrade pathing. Mitigation: per-lane waypoints for minions, distinct agent radii/types, flow-field escape hatch [S19][S36][S124].
- **Severity: medium - Skillshot dodge blind spots:** Riot's facing+cooldown heuristic fails on channeled and returning projectiles. Mitigation: per-ability dodge overrides, dodge-intent debug view [S28].
- **Severity: low - Unverified secondary details** (WR bot specifics, Dota Unfair vision claims, ~300ms tick excerpt, MLBB internals) kept provisional; do not build decisions on them [S88][S116][S83].
- **Severity: low - Chinese-ecosystem learn-from references** add friction (zhihu/QQ docs, ET framework coupling) [S97][S98][S106].
- **Severity: low - Save snapshot could miss a field;** versioned DTO with tolerant loading handles it; scope decided at Q10 [S124].
- **Severity: low - Platform ambiguity wastes phase 1 and 12 work** if input abstraction is deferred; dual-bound action maps from phase 1 mitigate [S2][S3].
