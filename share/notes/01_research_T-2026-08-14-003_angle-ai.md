## Task in one sentence

Research what the bot AI for a local single-player LoL-style MOBA needs in Unity (lane behavior, last hitting, ability usage, skillshots, teamfights, allied AI, difficulty dials, architecture) with verified, access-dated sources so any agent can implement it without re-researching.

## What we know (verified)

- **Difficulty dials, Dota 2 style (the reference table).** The Dota 2 wiki documents bot difficulties as a dial matrix: Passive (no abilities, no last hitting, no reactions, no group formation), Easy (400ms last-hit uncertainty, 200ms reaction time, group formation of 3, no deny/gank/stun-lock/channel-interrupt/illusion detection), Hard (50ms reaction, group of 5, all advanced behaviors on: stun-overlap avoidance, denying, harassing, ganking, channeling interruption, projectile dodging, illusion detection), Unfair (no reaction delay; +25% gold and XP per player reports; full-map vision claims on forums - see Risks R1) [S1][S14]. Medium row values (200ms last-hit uncertainty, 100ms reaction, group of 4, deny only in practice mode) come from the wiki table excerpt and difficulty text (table row was truncated on fetch) [S1].
- **LoL difficulty = scan-rate throttling + spell/item usage.** Riot's own dev blog: Beginner bots scan the environment at roughly 1/3 the previous frequency; Intermediate bots scan 50-100% as often depending on how threatened they are [S5]. LoL difficulties are Intro (default for new players since V4.12), Beginner, Intermediate; Intermediate bots can use Flash (V4.7) and are the only ones adding to custom games (V4.7) [S3]. Newer LoL AI (V14.6+) runs on "an entirely new framework ... more nuanced definitions and decision-making algorithms"; V14.12 added a role-assignment dropdown (multiple bots may share a role) and Barrier/Teleport usage [S3].
- **Skillshot dodge does NOT need projectile vision.** Riot's production heuristic (2014): track the opponent's facing direction + recently-used ability cooldowns; when a skillshot is detected and the caster faces the bot, dodge. Documented failure cases: Anivia's Flash Frost (no cooldown until travel completes) and Ahri's Orb of Deception return trip [S5]. This is the practical, no-netcode pattern for the reactive layer.
- **Skillshot casting = lead prediction math (well-documented).** Intercept time from relative position/velocity: solve |pRel + vt*t| = s*t (quadratic), aim at target_pos + vt*t; fixed-point iteration (2-3 passes) converges for constant-velocity targets; gravity-aware variant adds 0.5*g*t^2 lift [S11][S12][S13]. Game Developer's 2009 article covers the same derivation with the "dramatic misses" trick (pseudo-random aim perturbation) for skilled vs unskilled NPC feel [S11].
- **Threat evaluation is health + available spells, not damage-received.** Riot replaced the FPS-style "recent damage taken" model: bots evaluate their own and enemies' health AND spell availability (a drained Veigar is a target, not a threat). Spell damage is estimated roughly (the game does not pre-compute spell effects) - bots over- and under-commit like players [S5].
- **Lane behavior.** LoL bots historically assign 1-1-1-2 (solo top, solo mid, duo bottom) and can swap lanes/gank; one bot is designated jungler with Smite (kills jungle monsters, ganks lanes) since the V4.5-era AI work; bots group to kill epic monsters [S3][S4]. Bots may push a lane with three champions within the first 10 minutes [S4]. Dota bots secure bounty runes at start, always carry a Town Portal Scroll, and return to lane during the laning phase [S1].
- **Ally AI exists and is documented in Dota practice mode.** In Dota 2 practice/single-player, allied heroes are the same bot scripts; Medium bots "deny creeps" only in practice mode (allied side), i.e., allied bots run the same brain with role-specific flags [S1]. Fairness gesture: if an allied human disconnects, a bot on the OPPOSING team returns to fountain and stops playing until the player returns [S1].
- **Team-level coordination via pings/orders.** Dota bots respond to allied-player pings: ping allied structure -> all bots defend; ping enemy structure -> group in that lane to mass-push; ping enemy hero -> nearby bots abandon everything to kill that hero (even following into fountain); ping Roshan -> "Doing Roshan!" and group up [S1]. After laning, Hard/Unfair bots stick together as a group of 5 pushing/defending in unison, occasionally splitting into two groups to push two lanes, rejoining if one group is driven off [S1].
- **MLBB difficulty ladder (mobile MOBA reference).** AI Simple / AI Normal / AI Hard. Hard bots know when to use battle spells, time skills to kill, attack turrets correctly (lower difficulties die to turrets voluntarily), understand macro, follow the team, and escape properly [S6]. This is the cleanest three-rung ladder text for a mobile-style MOBA.
- **Unity Behavior (official package) is a live 2026 BT option.** `com.unity.behavior` v1.0.16 documents behavior graphs with four node types (action, modifier, sequencing, join); modifiers include Repeat, Inverter, Succeeder, Abort, Restart (conditional, with "Aborts if: Any/All Are True"); sequencing includes Sequence, Random, Try In Order, Run In Parallel [S7]. Official package with a visual editor - the current first-party BT tool for Unity.
- **Research corpus for MOBA agent architecture (context, not code).** Silva & Chaimowicz: a two-layered agent (navigation + game mechanics) using Influence Maps, tested in LoL [S9]; a Tutor Agent that plays alongside a novice player and gives tips [S10]; Font & Mahlmann: the Dota 2 Bot Competition framework for 1v1 mid bot AI research [S8]. Tencent's Honor-of-Kings RL line (full-MOBA RL, supervised human-level play, macro-goal diverse policies) is the state of the art for RL opponents - explicitly deferred per the prior ADR (ML-Agents trains Mono-only; RL/LLM not viable for local offline v1) [S16][prior ADR `share/notes/01_research_T-2026-08-14-002_angle-ai.md:44`].

## What we don't know (ambiguities + clarifying questions)

1. **Paid vs free AI tooling.** Unity Behavior is free (official); Behavior Designer / NodeCanvas are paid with visual editors. Changes the build-vs-reuse answer for the BT layer. *(carried from T-2026-08-14-002; re-ask for the MOBA)*
   - **Question:** "Is spending on paid Asset Store AI tools acceptable (Behavior Designer / NodeCanvas), or must everything be free (Unity Behavior, hand-rolled)?"
2. **Difficulty philosophy.** May the AI cheat (bonus gold/XP, map vision) on the top difficulty like Dota's Unfair, or must difficulty come only from reaction speed + decision quality (LoL's scan-rate model)?
   - **Question:** "On high difficulty, is it acceptable for the AI to get hidden advantages (extra gold/XP, vision), or should difficulty come only from faster reactions and smarter play?"
3. **Lane layout and jungle.** The user says "3-lane map": is it 1-1-1-2 (duo bottom, no jungle role) or 1-1-1-1-1 with a roaming jungler (like modern LoL)? This changes the lane-assignment table and whether a jungle-camping bot is in scope [S3][S4].
   - **Question:** "For the 3-lane map: duo bottom lane (1-1-1-2) or a dedicated jungler/roamer (1-1-1-1-1)?"
4. **Skillshot density.** The facing+cooldown dodge heuristic covers standard skillshots but fails on channeled (Anivia-type) and returning (Ahri-type) projectiles [S5]. If the hero kit has many such abilities, the dodge layer needs per-ability rules.
   - **Question:** "Does the ability design rely mostly on standard projectiles, or are there channeled/returning/ground-targeted skillshots that need per-ability dodge rules?"
5. **Unity version target.** Unity Behavior v1.0.16 availability and BT asset compatibility depend on the Unity version. *(carried from T-2026-08-14-002)*
   - **Question:** "Which Unity version will the project use (Unity 6 LTS, or another)?"

## Risks and doubts

- **R1 - Wiki/forum-derived difficulty numbers are a starting point, not a spec.** The Dota difficulty table is wiki-maintained (Medium row truncated on fetch; Unfair +25% gold/XP and map vision come from Steam/Reddit/GameFAQs posts, not Valve) [S1][S14]. LoL scan-rate figures come from a 2014 blog; current LoL AI is a different (V14.6+) framework [S5][S3].
  - **Severity:** high
  - **Mitigation:** implement every dial as ScriptableObject data (numbers, not code); treat all values as v1 starting points to be tuned in playtests; mark the tuning pass as an explicit build task.
- **R2 - Skillshot dodge heuristic has documented blind spots.** Riot's own caveats (channeled, returning, travel-time-cooldown abilities) mean the dodge layer can feel unfair or dumb depending on the ability kit [S5].
  - **Severity:** medium
  - **Mitigation:** build the dodge layer behind an interface with per-ability overrides; add a "dodge intent" debug view; decide per-ability coverage at design time (Q4).
- **R3 - Unity Behavior is a young official package (v1.0.16).** Behavior graphs are documented and first-party, but the package is newer than Behavior Designer/NodeCanvas; API or version churn risk over a long build [S7].
  - **Severity:** medium
  - **Mitigation:** keep the AI decision core behind an interface (swap BT backend without touching hero logic); pin the package version at plan time; verify with `chub` before the coder writes against it.
- **R4 - Dota API tick detail (GetDesire ~300ms) is from a search excerpt.** The Valve wiki page (developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Dota_Bot_Scripting) is Anubis-blocked for direct fetch; the 3-level structure (Team -> Mode -> Action, GetDesire per mode, Think per frame) and ~300ms desire evaluation cadence come from search snippets, not a full-text read [S2].
  - **Severity:** low
  - **Mitigation:** treat ~300ms as a ballpark starting tick for the tactical layer (consistent with the 0.1-0.3s tactical budget from the prior dossier); verify against the wiki via Jina Reader (`r.jina.ai/<url>`) at plan time before quoting it in the build spec.

## Technical findings (the 6 requested answers)

**F1. Hero brain: three-layer hierarchy, Dota API as the reference shape.** Strategy layer (team-level goals: lane, farm, roam, push, defend, roshan - the Dota mode list), tactical layer (mode selection with scored desires evaluated on a ~300ms cadence - GetDesire in Dota terms), reactive layer (per-frame action: move, attack, cast, item use - Think in Dota terms) [S2 snippet][S1]. LoL's threat-evaluation sits in the tactical layer (health + spell availability scoring) [S5]. Map to Unity: Behavior graphs (official package) for the tactical/reactive tree with Abort nodes for threat interrupts [S7]; utility scoring for continuous trade-offs (target selection, retreat thresholds) per prior RTS F13 [prior dossier `share/notes/01_research_T-2026-08-14-002_angle-ai.md:89`]. Influence-map layers (danger/objective maps) are the academic reference for the tactical layer [S9]. No GOAP needed for MOBA heroes (fixed ability kits, no tool-use planning) - per prior dossier F11 the GOAP fit was for build logic, which MOBAs do not have; the reactive tree + utility scorers cover it.

**F2. Lane behavior: assignment table + last-hitting dial.** Data-driven assignment: 1-1-1-2 (duo bot) with an optional jungler role carrying Smite [S3][S4]; player picks lane layout per Q3. Laning phase behaviors: farm with last-hit timing dial (Dota: 400ms -> 200ms -> 50ms uncertainty by difficulty) [S1], harass (Hard+), deny (Hard+; allied deny only in practice mode) [S1], retreat-from-early-aggression (Medium and below move away from close enemies) [S1], and post-laning rotation (LoL: lane swaps, ganks, 3-champ pushes in the first 10 minutes) [S4]. Implement as a lane-state FSM per hero (goToLane / farm / harass / deny / rotate) fed by the perception layer's last-seen data.

**F3. Combat behavior + difficulty dials.** Casting: lead-prediction aim (intercept-time quadratic or 2-3 fixed-point iterations; gravity term if projectiles arc) [S11][S12][S13]; add pseudo-random aim perturbation ("dramatic misses") scaled by difficulty for fair feel [S11]. Dodging: facing + cooldown-tracking heuristic, with per-ability overrides for channeled/returning projectiles [S5] (Q4). Reaction dials: Dota's 200ms (Easy) -> 100ms (Medium) -> 50ms (Hard) -> 0 (Unfair) [S1][S14]; LoL's scan-rate throttle (Beginner 1/3, Intermediate 50-100% by threat) [S5]. Micro dials (Hard+): stun-overlap avoidance, channeling interruption, projectile dodging, illusion detection [S1]. Top-difficulty dials: +25% gold/XP and (per forums) full-map vision - the "cheating" dial from prior F7, gated behind the user's answer to Q2 [S14][prior F7].

**F4. Allied AI: same brain, role flags, follow-player behaviors.** Evidence: Dota practice mode allies ARE the same bot scripts with mode restrictions (deny only for allies) [S1]; the disconnect fairness gesture (opposing bot stands down) [S1]; the Tutor Agent concept (AI plays alongside the player, gives tips) as an optional assist layer [S10]; MLBB's Hard AI "follows the team" - the allied-bot version is "follow the player's intent" (respond to pings: defend structure, group-push lane, kill pinged hero, objective) [S1][S6]. Recommendation: one hero-brain asset for both teams; ally instances add (a) ping/order listeners, (b) a "don't steal the player's farm" bias (stay off the player's lane last hits unless ordered), (c) assist-on-engagement triggers. The "annoying ally" failure mode is the design constraint here - follow orders, then back off.

**F5. Team-level coordination: ping-driven objectives + post-laning deathball.** Dota: bots react to player pings (defend structure / mass-push lane / focus hero / Roshan) [S1]; Hard/Unfair form a group of 5 after laning, pushing/defending in unison, splitting into 2 groups for dual-lane pushes and rejoining when driven off [S1]; LoL: bots group for epic monsters, jungler ganks [S3][S4]; MLBB Hard: macro + follow team [S6]. Implement as a team-strategy tick (0.5-2s cadence, per prior F20) that computes: current mode (laneFarm / groupPush / defend / objective / retreat), target lanes, and group-formation size dial (3/4/5) [S1][prior F20].

**F6. Performance/architecture at ~70-80 entities.** Budget: 10 heroes (5v5) + ~60-70 minions/towers. Minions need only a lightweight FSM (lane -> attack nearest -> push), not the full hero brain [design guidance, cheap-FSM per prior F9]. Hero brains: strategy tick 0.5-2s, tactical tick ~0.1-0.3s (Dota's ~300ms GetDesire cadence matches), reactive per-frame/event-driven [S2 snippet][prior F20]. Scan throttling is the documented production technique to keep per-frame cost flat (Riot throttled scan frequency per difficulty and shipped "without affecting server load") [S5]. Coroutine ticks with cached yield instructions per prior dossier S9 [prior `share/notes/01_research_T-2026-08-14-002_angle-ai.md:103`]. No DOTS needed at this entity count (prior core ADR). Perception-first: all bot decisions read an AI view model fed by the same fog-of-war the player uses (prior F21) - this is what makes difficulty dials (vision) enforceable [prior F21 `share/notes/01_research_T-2026-08-14-002_angle-ai.md:105`].
