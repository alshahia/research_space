## What we know for sure (consolidated)

### Platform (angle A)
- Kotobee = product family of **Vijua** (Egypt, founded 2011). Products: **Kotobee Author** (interactive ebook editor), **Reader** (free apps), **Cloud** (hosted DRM delivery, $10–$500/mo), **Library** (branded bookshelf, $100–$1,000/mo), **Narrator** (free MP3→audiobook), **Kotobee Books** (free self-publishing storefront). (https://www.kotobee.com/)
- **Kotobee Press is dead. The current author storefront is `https://books.kotobee.com/`** (formerly "Kotobee Shared Library"; selling enabled Aug 2023). Verified: kotobee.com/press, press.kotobee.com, kotobee.com/kotobee-press all 404.
- **Kotobee Books economics:** free publishing, **100% royalties** ("for a limited time" per support article), no listing fees; author connects own **Stripe or PayPal** keys; payments go directly to the author. Formats **EPUB/KPUB only**, max 500 MB/file, max 30 books/account, price in USD, cover 1600×2400 px (min width 1400). (support articles 8000120127, 8000111089)
- **Kotobee Author pricing:** Free license $0 lifetime → Basic $150 → Premium $300 → Institutional $2,000 (one-time). Built-in AI tools (text/image/question/TTS). Exports: EPUB, MOBI, PDF, Word, web/mobile apps, SCORM/LTI/xAPI. (kotobee.com/en/pricing/author, kotobee.com/academy)
- **Minimum start budget: $0.** Realistic year-1: $0–$450 (Premium + 1 cloud ebook). Cloud $10–$500/mo; Library $100–$1,000/mo (2019 table, confirm).
- **Audience:** "300,000+ customers" (marketing claim, unaudited); center of gravity is B2B education/training (Harvard, Yale, Pearson, Oracle, Aramco, MENA governments); Arabic site (كتبي) + Arabic samples + eFinance gateway (Egypt, v1.9.8, 2026-07) = first-class MENA support; Spanish site/blog too.
- **Comparison (new author):** Kotobee wins on 0% commission + interactivity + Arabic/MENA + no exclusivity; loses on discoverability (no marketplace traffic), no print, interactive-feature lock-in (plain EPUB/PDF exports are DRM-free and non-interactive).
- Free learning: 7-day email course (kotobee.com/ebook-publishing-basics-course), Academy (47 videos, kotobee.com/academy), blog (blog.kotobee.com), help center (support.kotobee.com).

## Risks and doubts (merged, top 5)

1. **Discoverability — Severity: high.** Kotobee Books is a small storefront with no public traffic data, no charts; 90% of self-pub books sell <100 copies. **Mitigation:** multi-channel (Kotobee + KDP + Apple + Google + own site); marketing is the differentiator; treat Kotobee as "100%-royalty interactive flagship," not sole channel.
2. **"100% royalties" time-limited — Severity: medium.** Support article says "for a limited time." **Mitigation:** capture terms at publish time; plan economics at 70% benchmark so the case survives.
3. **Kotobee-specific demand unmeasurable — Severity: medium.** Category counts are supply-side; demand triangulated from global data (flagged per figure). **Mitigation:** ranking weights Kotobee-fit explicitly; dual-lane advice.
4. **AI-content legal/ToS uncertainty — Severity: medium.** Kotobee Books ToS AI clause unread (link exists); US Copyright Office requires human authorship; KDP requires AI disclosure. **Mitigation:** AI-assisted (human-directed) workflows; disclosure checklist; verify ToS at signup.
5. **Format/DRM lock-in — Severity: low.** Interactivity only inside Kotobee Reader/Cloud/Library apps; plain EPUB exports are non-interactive. **Mitigation:** keep Markdown/DOCX masters; export plain EPUB for retail; reserve interactive versions for Kotobee channels.
