## Task in one sentence

Determine which book genres actually read/sell on Kotobee (kotobee.com + the Kotobee Books storefront) and globally, so the user — likely an Arabic-speaking self-publisher — can pick what to write/publish on Kotobee; produce a ranked, sorted, link-cited genre shortlist with examples and resources.

**Scope note:** all URLs accessed/verified live on 2026-08-12 unless stated. Every figure carries its source. Where Kotobee-specific sales data does not exist, global ebook-market data is used and explicitly flagged as triangulation.

## What we know for sure

- Kotobee is primarily an **ebook-authoring/distribution platform** (Author, Reader, Cloud, Library, Narrator products), trusted by "over 300,000 customers" (homepage, accessed 2026-08-12, https://www.kotobee.com/). Its four named solution verticals are **K12 Education, Higher Education, Corporate Training, Digital Publishing** — the entire sales architecture is education/training-first.
- Kotobee's **consumer storefront is `books.kotobee.com`** ("Kotobee Books — Free Self Publishing Platform"): free publishing, **100% royalties**, 3-step publish flow, reader-facing library at `/library` (accessed 2026-08-12, https://books.kotobee.com/). The old `kotobee.com/kotobee-press` / `press.kotobee.com` paths are dead (sibling angle confirmed; this angle verified the replacement is live).
- The Kotobee Books library exposes **24 categories with live book counts** (rendered via browser snapshot of https://books.kotobee.com/library, 2026-08-12). Supply ranking (book counts): Educational **3589** > Classics/Literature & Fiction **1758** > History & Geography **1287** > Arts & Photography **613** > Children's Books **503** > Mystery & Thriller **456** > Short Stories **433** > Audiobooks **430** > Cooking **413** > Biographies & Memoirs **377** > Health & Wellbeing **353** > Fantasy **320** > Engineering & Technology **299** > Science Fiction & Supernatural **241** > Humor & Entertainment **214** > Psychology & Self-Help **214** > Horror **147** > Poetry **110** > Politics & Social Sciences **91** > Society & Culture **68** > Sports **62** > Comics & Magazines **58** > Travel **44**.
- The storefront taxonomy has **no Romance category and no Religion/Spirituality category** (largest global fiction genre and a major nonfiction category are structurally absent there) — a meaningful gap finding (same snapshot).
- Observed storefront price points: **$1–$15**, typical $3–$10 (e.g., Tasty Treats Diabetic Cookbook $1, DARKNESS $3, The Golden Oath $5, The Circle $6, Time Mastery $9, The Hidden Weight of Stress $10, Execution Line – With Workbook $15, MySQL Expert – Complete Certification $15 — all rendered in the same snapshot).
- Kotobee's own 17 sample ebooks (https://www.kotobee.com/en/samples, 2026-08-12) sort into Education & Training (7), Lifestyle & Wellness (5), Creative Writing (3), Arts & Design (2) — and include **Arabic titles** (مرض الزهايمر / Alzheimer's, لغة الجسد / Body Language), **Spanish titles** (El Leon y El Raton, Yoga En Casa), and **children's audiobooks** (The Great Marshmallow Chase, The Cat Who Knew Secrets).
- Kotobee case studies (https://www.kotobee.com/en/case-studies, 2026-08-12) are dominated by education/training/government: Virginia Tech interactive 5G textbook, Digital SAT prep, Ain Shams University (Egypt) ASU2Learn library, Egypt's **Ketab national digital library**, **Zedne Arabic children's ebooks library**, travel guides (Venice, Florence, Zara Khanna), fitness (Yogoskenz), music (Piano for All), plus business-training cases (MedEducation +10% margin, Wicked Copters −35% costs, TaF.tc −15%, Salvamento DRM).
- Kotobee runs a **fully localized Arabic site** (كتبي) with Arabic samples, Arabic testimonials (e.g., Sultan Qaboos University), and an Arabic education whitepaper (`/files/docs/education-whitepaper-ar.pdf`) — accessed 2026-08-12, https://www.kotobee.com/ar. Spanish site and Spanish blog also exist (https://www.kotobee.com/es, https://blog.kotobee.com/es/).
- Global market data verified live (all WordsRated pages carry an "updated 2026-03-27" stamp): romance = **$1.44B, highest-earning fiction genre** (60% of its units are ebooks; 50% of the most competitive Kindle genres are romance); thriller/mystery = **12.5% of adult books sold**, #2 most profitable Amazon category 2022, #2 audiobook genre; fantasy+SF = **$590.2M/yr US** with +45.3% growth in 2021 and the largest audiobook share; self-help = **$10.5B industry (2020), +19.3% sales growth 2021**, and all *least*-competitive Kindle genres are nonfiction; religious books = **$757.7M US sales 2022 (+7.46%)** but only 12.5% digital (ebook 6.9% + audiobook 5.6%); children's = **32.8% of all US book sales (2022)**; educational publishing = **31.28% of publishing-industry revenue (2022)**.
- K-lytics (https://k-lytics.com/, accessed 2026-08-12) is **live and fully operational** — a paid Kindle market-intelligence subscription (BASIC/PREMIUM/ELITE, 30 main categories, 400+ subcategories, 2,400+ sub-subcategories, proprietary Category Power Index). This **contradicts the sibling angle note that the domain is "for sale"** — flagging for master's merge step.

## What we don't know (ambiguities)

- Kotobee publishes no public sales/revenue data per genre or per book. The category counts above are **supply-side** (books listed), not demand-side (books sold). Demand must be triangulated from global data + Kotobee's own marketing choices.
  - **Suggested clarifying question:** "Do you want us to rank genres primarily by (a) what sells globally (fiction-heavy: romance/thriller/fantasy), or (b) what Kotobee structurally supports best (interactive educational/children's/self-help)? We recommend a blend, but your writing strengths decide the weight."
- Whether Kotobee Books storefront has **paid promotion/discoverability** tools for authors is unverified this session (no ads/featured-books program found on /library, /write, or the FAQ; sibling platform angle may cover it).
  - **Suggested clarifying question:** "Do you plan to sell ONLY on the Kotobee Books storefront, or also export to Amazon/Kobo/your own branded store? (Kotobee exports 12+ formats; this changes genre advice.)"
- Arabic-market ebook demand data is not available from any fetched source; Arabic evidence here is qualitative (Kotobee's Arabic ecosystem, Egyptian/Gulf customers, Arabic storefront titles).
  - **Suggested clarifying question:** "Is your primary target audience Arabic-speaking (MENA), English-speaking, or both? This changes the recommended genre mix (Arabic skews educational/children's/faith/self-development; English skews fiction)."
- Kotobee Books "views/rating" sort exists in the library UI (Sort by Views/Rating options observed) but per-book view counts require clicking individual books; not harvested this session.
  - **Suggested clarifying question:** none — planner may harvest top-viewed titles per category in a later pass if needed.

## Risks and doubts

- **Kotobee-specific demand is unmeasurable; the ranking leans on supply-side + global data.** If Kotobee storefront traffic is small, "what sells on Kotobee Books" may be dominated by off-platform distribution (authors who export to Amazon/Kobo and use Kotobee only as a tool).
  - **Severity:** high
  - **Mitigation:** ranking weights Kotobee-fit (interactivity/DRM/Arabic leverage) explicitly per genre, and the plan should advise dual-lane publishing (Kotobee storefront + Amazon/Kobo export) rather than single-lane.
- **Global figures are US-centric and dated 2017–2023 even where the page was refreshed 2026-03-27** (WordsRated pages reuse older underlying surveys; e.g., author-income data is 2017). 2024–2026 genre-revenue splits could not be verified from AAP/Statista this session (Statista paywalled, not fetched).
  - **Severity:** medium
  - **Mitigation:** each figure is cited with its underlying vintage; planner should treat genre ranking as stable-over-3-years (romance/thriller/self-help/children's dominance has persisted), not as a 2026 snapshot.
- **Sibling-angle contradiction (K-lytics "for sale")** — if master merged without the live check, the dossier would wrongly drop the best Kindle niche-research tool.
  - **Severity:** low
  - **Mitigation:** verified live this session; explicitly flagged in this report for the merge pass.

## Language / market lens (English vs Spanish vs Arabic on Kotobee)

**English (largest, most competitive):** Adult fiction = 41% of all ebook sales (WordsRated, 2021 data); fiction genres (romance/thriller/fantasy) sell best but face Kindle saturation; English nonfiction (self-help, how-to, education) is the Kotobee-native lane. BookTok is the discovery engine (Kotobee blog covers it). Advice: English lane = interactive nonfiction/children's on Kotobee storefront; fiction exported to Amazon.

**Spanish (Kotobee's second full market):** Kotobee maintains a full Spanish site, Spanish blog, Spanish samples (El Leon y El Raton children's fable, Yoga En Casa), Spanish-language case studies (Universidad La Salle Oaxaca language learning, UAEMex interactive study guides, CREASI training), and Spanish books on the storefront (Carlos Arroba's @DESPIERTA TU ALMA, El Canon Olvidado). Children's + study guides + self-development mirror the English lane; US Hispanic + LatAm markets are reachable via the same storefront.

**Arabic (Kotobee's structural differentiator):** Evidence is strong and qualitative: full كتبي Arabic site + Arabic whitepaper, Arabic samples, Arabic testimonials (Sultan Qaboos University), MENA institutional customers (KSA/Oman/Qatar/UAE/Egypt governments, Ain Shams, Helwan, al-Qasemi), Arabic publishers (Elyssar Press, Rushd Bookstore, Minhaj), Arabic user books on the storefront (الدليل الشامل للسيارات الكهربائية والهجينة, مدرسة المستقبل, ملحمة سفينة الفكر وحقيقة فلسطين, plus Kotobee's own Arabic education titles), and the Ketab national digital library (Egypt). No quantitative Arabic ebook-market data was found this session (flagged). Genre implication: Arabic demand concentrates in **education/children's/self-development/faith**; Arabic fiction (esp. romance) is publishable but should stay "clean/family" for MENA retail norms (market-norm judgment, flagged). Kotobee's Arabic RTL + DRM + mobile reader gives Arabic self-publishers a native toolchain that KDP's Arabic support cannot match (KDP Arabic is functional but Kotobee's interactive/RTL-first design is its home turf — Kotobee was built with Arabic as a first-class language).

**How this changes the recommendation:** a monolingual Arabic author should lead with children's + educational + self-development in Arabic (ranks 1–3, 8 in the table); an English-first author should lead with interactive nonfiction/self-help/children's on Kotobee + fiction export to Amazon; a bilingual EN/AR author can dual-release the same interactive template in both languages at near-zero marginal cost (Kotobee samples prove the dual-language pattern).
