## What we know for sure

- **US comprehensive sanctions on Syria were revoked effective 2025-07-01** (E.O. of June 30, 2025, #14312; six founding EOs revoked; 31 CFR 542 Syrian Sanctions Regulations removed from CFR). Sanctions now remaining are **targeted** (Assad + associates, HTS, Captagon, Iran-linked, etc.) under the renamed PAARSS program (31 CFR 569). Syria is not a comprehensively sanctioned country as of the access date. [https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-programs-and-country-information/paarss, 2026-08-12]
- **Iraq is not comprehensively sanctioned either**: the comprehensive Iraqi Sanctions Regulations were removed in 2010 (75 FR 55462); only the targeted "Iraq Stabilization and Insurgency Sanctions Regulations" (31 CFR 576) remains, aimed at persons threatening stabilization. [https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-programs-and-country-information/iraq-related-sanctions, 2026-08-12]
- OFAC administers **no single "country list"** — programs are targeted/SDN-based; a person's location in Iraq or Syria is not, by itself, a prohibition under OFAC. [https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-programs-and-country-information/where-is-ofacs-country-list-what-countries-do-i-need-to-worry-about-in-terms-of-us-sanctions, 2026-08-12]
- **Neither Iraq nor Syria has a US income-tax treaty** (verified by absence from the IRS treaty A–Z list; Egypt has one). Consequence: no treaty-based reduction of US withholding for either citizenship or residence. [https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z, 2026-08-12]
- US-source royalties to foreign persons are taxed at **30% by default**, withheld at source under IRC §1441; W-8BEN Line 2 asks **country of citizenship**; Line 3 asks permanent/tax-residence address. [https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben, 2026-08-12]
- KDP itself documents: "The default withholding rate is 30%, but you may be eligible for a reduced rate if your country of tax residence has a tax treaty with the U.S." [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274750, 2026-08-12]
- **KDP payment methods are chosen by bank-account location.** Iraq and Syria are NOT on KDP's payment-options country list (list includes: US, CA, MX, BR, ZA, Egypt, Morocco, Tunisia, Kenya, Nigeria, IN, JP, AU, NZ, HK, MY, SG, TH, ID, PH, PK, Israel, Jordan, EU, UK …). For banks in unlisted countries the "Other" table shows **paper check by mail only** (USD), and Amazon.pl shows no check either. [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GJD7DFFX6X88AK46, 2026-08-12]
- KDP runs a **Payment Service Provider (PSP) program**; as of **2026-08-01** payments to non-participating PSPs stopped. **Payoneer and Wise are both on the participating-PSP list** (also Airwallex, WorldFirst, PingPong, etc.). PSP eligibility is per-account (KDP notifies if only a deposit-taking bank is allowed). [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GD9NN4LS8ZDU8XZ7 and G6GLVRHVQZY4V4FX, 2026-08-12]
- KDP account creation is **address-based** (mailing address for tax + royalty payments) with an identity-verification step; the tax profile generates the W-8 (citizenship enters via the W-8). KDP help does not publish a list of eligible sign-up countries — FLAG. [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200620010, 2026-08-12]
- **Google Play Books: Iraq and Syria are absent from BOTH the seller-signup column and the Payments column** of the official supported-countries table. Google's payment rule: "You must have a local business address and local bank account in at least one of the countries supported for payment" and "At this time, Google doesn't offer alternative payment methods." (South Africa shows the split: sellers OK, payments ✘.) [https://support.google.com/books/partner/answer/2987594 and /answer/6009580, 2026-08-12]

Patreon bank transfers are processed via Payoneer. Patreon explicitly notes: "As long as you live in a country supported by Payoneer, you can still register your bank account, even if it's located in a different country" and that a non-US creator may select the US as bank country for USD payouts (documented option, not a documented right). ## Risks and doubts

- **Payoneer-account-eligibility is the single gating fact for the largest cluster of platforms** (KDP via PSP, D2D, Patreon, many creator platforms). If Payoneer no longer onboards Iraq residents, the entire Payoneer-dependent cluster collapses to check-by-mail or blocked. **Severity:** high — **Mitigation:** test Payoneer registration now (free, minutes); it is the cheapest possible de-risking step.
- **PayPal non-availability in Iraq/Syria** kills Lulu (PayPal-or-check), Ko-fi (PayPal-or-Stripe), Smashwords-class rails and any PayPal-only payout. **Severity:** high — **Mitigation:** test PayPal signup with KRG address; confirm whether the author has an existing PayPal account from another country (and the compliance posture of using it).
- **Stripe non-availability in Iraq/Syria** blocks Substack, Kotebee Books (Stripe leg), Ko-fi (Stripe leg), Gumroad-class merchant rails. **Severity:** high — **Mitigation:** none direct; treat Stripe-dependent platforms as blocked unless a supported-country entity/bank account is available (user-side, gray area — see Flags).
- **30% US withholding is unavoidable** (no treaty for Iraq or Syria) on US-sourced royalties from KDP/D2D/Lulu/Apple unless delivery-based exceptions apply (Lulu documents a US-delivery-only withholding scope; Apple/KDP/Google do not). This is a margin fact, not a blocker. **Severity:** medium — **Mitigation:** price books accordingly; keep W-8BEN current (expiry resets to 30%).
- **Policy volatility:** the July-2025 Syria sanctions revocation is recent and the PAARSS regime is dynamic; platform compliance teams may lag OFAC. A platform may still reject "Syria" in a form long after OFAC removed the program. **Severity:** medium — **Mitigation:** re-verify each platform's signup at the moment of registration; treat any platform rejection as appealable via its support channel.
- **State Sponsor of Terrorism designation remains for Syria** (the June-2025 EO text directs continued handling of the SST designation; no comprehensive OFAC ban follows from it, but some platform compliance policies treat SST countries restrictively beyond OFAC). **Severity:** medium — **Mitigation:** FLAG; test per platform.
- **KRG banking reality** (USD account availability, SWIFT reception, correspondent fees) is user-side and uncitable from platform docs. **Severity:** medium — **Mitigation:** user checks with their bank first; several rails assume a working USD account.
- **Gray-area workarounds** (US bank country on Patreon, Wise USD account as KDP PSP, Stripe Atlas US incorporation) are documented features in some cases but carry account-risk/compliance uncertainty for a Syrian national; not endorsed, merely flagged. **Severity:** high (account risk) — **Mitigation:** discuss with the user before any workaround; do not recommend as primary path.

## Tax (cited)

- **W-8BEN**: required by US platforms for non-US individuals; Line 2 = **country of citizenship**, Line 3 = permanent residence (tax residence); without a valid W-8 → 30% withholding (foreign-person rate, IRC §1441). [https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben, 2026-08-12]
- **No US–Iraq and no US–Syria income tax treaty** (IRS treaty A–Z; Egypt listed, Iraq/Syria not). → **30% US withholding on US-sourced royalties applies to this author** regardless of which country is written on the W-8BEN. [https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z, 2026-08-12]
- KDP: default 30%; treaty reduction only; W-8 expiry → back to 30%; TIN can be Iraq's national TIN, ITIN or EIN. [https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274750, 2026-08-12]
- D2D: "required to withhold 30% income tax for all international publishers … by default"; treaty via online W-8 interview. [https://www.draft2digital.com/faq/, 2026-08-12]
- Lulu: withholds 30% (foreign, no W-8BEN) **only on sales delivered within the US** for Lulu-ISBN royalties; own-ISBN revenue = "Other Revenue", no US withholding; 1042-S for non-US; 1099-K US-only context. [https://help.lulu.com/en/support/solutions/articles/64000255468-tax-and-withholding-the-basics, 2026-08-12]
- Apple: US tax form required for ALL partners (W-9 US / W-8BEN etc. non-US); country-specific forms for AU/CA/BR/IE/MX; **Ireland exporter-status (no Irish VAT on Apple purchases) ended April 2026** — verified change on Apple's own page. [https://itunespartner.apple.com/books/support/7-required-tax-forms, 2026-08-12]
- Google Play: US tax info required when selling in US/USD; 1099-K/1042-S mechanics via Google's tax help. [https://support.google.com/books/partner/answer/6009580 and /answer/10722804 (FAQ: Submit your US tax information), 2026-08-12]
- **"Tax residency" for a Syrian national in KRG — factual only**: tax residency is a country-of-residence concept (Iraq taxes residents); for US withholding purposes what matters is the treaty country on the W-8BEN — none applies for Iraq or Syria, so the outcome is 30% either way. Gray areas: (a) whether the author is an Iraq tax resident at all (KRG/Iraq tax administration realities — not verifiable here), (b) TIN availability for Iraq on platform forms (KDP accepts "your country's TIN", ITIN, or EIN), (c) EU VAT / Canada GST mechanics for Kobo (Canada-based) and Google Ireland VAT (IE6388047V published) — no per-author impact found for a KRG author, FLAG.
